# What The Next Cigar could borrow from Cigar Aficionado — and what it shouldn't

**15 September 2026.** Cigar Aficionado's site, read today, is built on eight things: a ratings database (23,000+ scores, searchable by origin, score and date), a bi-monthly magazine, Cigar of the Week, the annual Top 25, Big Smoke events, a Where to Smoke directory, video/podcasts, and two paid email products (Cigar Insider, Cigar Watch). It sells the authority of the score. We can't and shouldn't compete on scores — we don't sell tobacco and we don't pretend to a palate we haven't earned — but every one of those eight has a Next Cigar equivalent that runs on data we already have.

| Cigar Aficionado has | The Next Cigar equivalent | Have it? | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ratings database, searchable | **The board**: 52 vitolas × 23 retailers, live prices, landed cost. Our "score" is the price at your door, which nobody else publishes. | Yes | — |
| Cigar of the Week (a score) | **Box of the week**: the vitola whose cheapest live box moved most against its 90-day median, with the reason. The mover already exists on the board; give it a name, a Monday slot and a carousel. | Half | 1 day |
| Top 25 of the year | **The 25 boxes that moved**: the year's biggest landed-price swings, with the retailer that caused each. Data piece, December. | No | 2 days, once |
| Where to Smoke | **The Lounge map**: 100+ venues, member-submitted. Currently behind login; a public read-only version (no members, just venues) is a search magnet ("cigar lounge Malmö"). | Half | 2 days |
| Cigar Watch (paid alerts) | **Price alerts**: free, per vitola, live. Already better than theirs; underpromoted. Put the bell on every board row, not only the SKU page. | Yes | ½ day |
| Cigar Insider (paid newsletter) | **The Weekend Edition**: Friday email — the mover, the board's three biggest spreads, one guide, one product. Free first; a paid tier only when there are photographs and verdicts to sell. | Draft exists | 1 day to wire |
| Big Smoke events | **A herf**: one evening, Malmö or Copenhagen, Lounge members, a venue from the map. Don't build an events business; host one and photograph it. | No | An evening |
| Video / podcasts | **/watch** aggregates seven channels; add our own reels (the shot lists exist). | Half | — |
| Cigar 101 | Exists (14 pieces). Missing: one canonical "How to read a Cuban box" explainer that the decoder links to. | Yes | ½ day |
| Cigar Gear reviews | **The shop's "What we know" pages** — honest by design, but empty of verdicts until the parcels land. The photographs and verdicts are the whole difference between us and a drop-shipper. | Half | The parcels |

Three things they don't have that we can own: (1) **landed cost** — nobody in the category prices a box at the reader's door; (2) the **box-code decoder** that refuses to guess; (3) the **Habanos supply story**, told with numbers — today's Germany piece is the first; a monthly "Shelf report" (in stock / tracked, by country) is a series nobody else can run because nobody else reads the shelves four times a day.

What not to borrow: numeric scores, celebrity covers, "Cigar Life" luxury filler, and the paywall-first funnel. The paper's edge is that it says what it knows and where the number came from.
