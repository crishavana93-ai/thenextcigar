## 1. The supplier, honestly

**Guangzhou Xinkaituo Trading Co., Ltd.** — Alibaba store `cigarloong.en.alibaba.com`. Founded 2025, 1 year on Alibaba, "Multispecialty Supplier", verified by TÜV Rheinland, store rating 4.8/5 (57 ratings), response ≤2 h, on-time dispatch 99.4 %, reorder rate 14 %, online revenue US$180,000+. Main markets: US 24 %, Saudi 8 %, Canada 8 %, UAE 7 %, Iraq 6 %.

Three things that shape how to deal with them:

1. **They are a trading company, not the factory.** "Cooperated suppliers (2)" on their page says so. CIGARLOONG is a brand (it also sells on Amazon and AliExpress at retail), so you are buying branded goods through a reseller with MOQ 1. That is convenient and it caps your margin: the factory price is below theirs, and the retail price on Amazon is the ceiling your reader will compare against.
2. **Sales are real but modest.** Of 719 listings, 292 have any sales; the best genuine accessory has 597 orders, most "hot sellers" have 20–300. There is no 10,000-unit runaway product here except humidification tablets sold by the sheet. "Hot selling" is relative.
3. **Shipping is the good news.** Small items (≤0.7 kg) ship "Air + Express via FLY Logistics", quoted **SEK 39.93 per piece, transit est. 8–9 days** on every lighter, cutter and travel case I opened. Heavy ceramics quote SEK 120–160 per piece; the hygrometer ships from elsewhere at 17–24 days. So the 7–9-day promise you saw is accurate for the light accessories, on a single-piece air quote, not a pallet.

**The catch for Sweden:** you are the importer. Add 25 % Swedish VAT on (price + shipping), plus customs duty (lighters 2.7 %, cutters/knives ~0–8.5 %, leather cases 3–9.7 % depending on classification), plus the carrier's handling fee (PostNord/DHL, typically SEK 75–125 per parcel). A SEK 98 lighter with SEK 40 shipping lands around **SEK 250–280** per unit at single-piece quantities. Order 6+ of a line to spread the shipping and the fee; then the lighter lands nearer SEK 180.

## 6. What this list is not

It is one supplier's own sales counts, lifetime, on one marketplace. It says which of *their* products move, not which cigar accessories sell best in the world. To say that, we would compare against at least two other stores (Alibaba's search pages hit a CAPTCHA today, which I do not solve) and against Amazon EU best-seller ranks in "cigar accessories". Worth doing before a large order; not needed before a six-piece sample.
