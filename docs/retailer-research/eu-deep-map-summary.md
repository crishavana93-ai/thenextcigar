## Eight headline corrections vs. my earlier fabricated summary

These corrections actually matter for the country-config + scraper sprint:

1. **France has ZERO active LCDH locations.** My fabrication and the brief both implied LCDH presence in France. None exist — historic Boulevard Saint-Germain Paris LCDH is no longer in the active franchise registry; Cannes/Saint-Tropez/Lyon LCDH never existed.
2. **France's Habanos importer is Coprova SAS** (founded December 1970, Pomezia-equivalent role for FR + Monaco), NOT Seita / Imperial Tobacco France. Seita handles cigarettes, not Cubans.
3. **Portugal's Habanos importer is EMPOR S.A.** (founded April 1, 1960, headquartered Queluz/Lisbon), NOT Tabaqueira. Tabaqueira (PMI subsidiary) does cigarettes + IQOS only.
4. **Switzerland has 10 LCDH, not 11** — Basel, Zürich, Lugano, Mendrisio (opened 2024), St. Gallen, Zug, Samnaun, Nyon, Genève, Montreux.
5. **Switzerland has ~30 Habanos Specialists, not 13** — Intertabak/Habanos.com glossary current count.
6. **Italy has 3 LCDH, not 8** — Milano (Borla, Via Anfossi 28), Roma (Fincato, Via Colonna Antonina 34), Napoli (Sisimbro, Via San Pasquale 74). Brief overcounted.
7. **Spain has 4 verified LCDH, not 9+** — Madrid Recoletos, Tenerife Adeje, Gran Canaria Mogán Mall (opened May 2024), Palma de Mallorca. Press kits conflating Habanos Specialist estancos with LCDH franchises is widespread.
8. **Austria has 33 Habanos Specialists, not 25** — 5th Avenue marketing copy still cites the 2014 launch baseline; the live Standorte table shows 33. Vienna's heritage flagship is **Maria Mohilla on Kohlmarkt 6 (since 1692)**, NOT "House of Glanz" or "Klocker Tobacco" — those names from the brief don't match any verifiable retailer.

## Six legal archetypes (corrected and confirmed)

This is what the country-config table needs to encode. Without it the alert engine will fire emails into markets where it's illegal:

1. **Online-friendly, schema-rich** — DE, UK, CH, NL, SE, DK, IE, CZ, PL, LU, MC. Real e-commerce stacks (Shopware 6 in DE, Shopify in UK/scattered, Magento/PrestaShop in CH, WooCommerce in SE/DK/NL). This is where The Finder's price-comparison engine delivers actual value.
2. **Online sale fully prohibited** — FR (Art. 568 ter CGI), ES (Ley 28/2005), FI (Tobacco Act §65), BE (1977 law + 2022 amendment), IT-transnational (D.Lgs. 6/2016). Directory-only, no prices, no buy buttons.
3. **Cross-border distance ban** — NO (Tobakksskadeloven §21b, Jan 1 2026). Norwegians cannot legally order from EU retailers. Geofence Norway view to NO retailers only.
4. **Domestic grey-zone with strict geofence** — IT. Italian tabaccai operate WooCommerce catalogs domestically; transnational delivery to IT illegal; the Finder must geofence Italian listings to IT-IP users only.
5. **Display image banned** — DK (April 2021). Text-only SKU matching, no visual fingerprinting. Plus MitID payment gate since Oct 2024.
6. **State price-uniform** — AT (5th Avenue tariff), PT mainland (EMPOR tariff), IS (ÁTVR monopoly), ES (CMT/BOE fix), FR (JORF homologation), IT (ADM tariff). Directory + availability tracking, NOT price comparison.
