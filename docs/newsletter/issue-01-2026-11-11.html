01  ·  The Chen Zhi case
Viñales, Cuba. Photo: Bryan Ledgard / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0

A court could hand back 28.55% of Habanos. The sanctions stay.

On 21 October, Justice Gerhard Wallbank of the High Court of the British Virgin Islands said he will end the court-run liquidation of 30 companies linked to Chen Zhi. One of them holds his indirect stake in Habanos S.A., the company that sells Cuban cigars worldwide: 28.55%, which makes him its second-largest shareholder after the Cuban state.

The judge found the court had not been given admissible evidence to wind the companies up. "Mere association is not enough," he said. The ruling does not touch the US allegations, and it lifts none of the sanctions that have already stopped deliveries to parts of Europe.
