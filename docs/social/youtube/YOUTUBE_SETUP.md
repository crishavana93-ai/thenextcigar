## 3. Rules per upload
- **AI label:** set "Altered or synthetic content" to Yes, because the voice is AI.
- **Title:** a question hook, e.g. "Why do some cigar names exist twice? (Twin Names, Part 1)"
- **Description:** copy the caption and its sources. Link only to the article, never to the Finder or a price page.
- **Same rules as Instagram:** no prices, shops, buying advice, taste verdicts, lit cigars or packaging shots.
- **Brand names:** OK in history and news under the conditions in BRAND_NAMES_DUE_DILIGENCE.
- **Never run YouTube ads on these videos.** Google bans tobacco promotion in ads.
