1 · Air in the tank · how to bleed it
Step 1Turn the flame adjuster to its lowest setting.
Step 2Point the refill valve away from your face.
Step 3Press the valve with a small screwdriver until the hissing stops.
Step 4Refill.
Fix 1

Bleed it. Then refill.

Every refill lets in a little air, and over time the lighter pushes out air instead of gas. Xikar says to bleed before every refill.

2 · Wait after refilling · what each maker says
Colibri2 minutes
Zippo butane inserts2 minutes
Xikar3–4 minutes
Vector5–10 minutes
Fix 2

Don't light it straight away.

Liquid butane from the can is cold and the pressure needs time to settle. If gas sprays back while you fill, stop: it's full.

3 & 4 · Flame and jet
Too lowTurn the adjuster towards + a little at a time.
Too highLights, then blows itself out? Turn it down a notch.
Dirty jetWipe with a little alcohol, let it dry 2–3 minutes, then short bursts of low-pressure air.
Fixes 3 & 4

Set the flame. Clean the jet.

Low-grade butane leaves residue that clogs the jet. Xikar says it will damage a lighter.

5 · The cold · butane's boiling point
−0.5 °CNear freezing, butane barely turns to gas, so there's almost no pressure to push it out of the jet.
Fix 5

Warm it in your hand.

Colibri says to let it reach room temperature. Keep it in an inside pocket, not a coat or a cold car.

Safety

Never refill near a flame.

Hissing when you're not pressing means a leaking valve. Stop using it and send it for service.
