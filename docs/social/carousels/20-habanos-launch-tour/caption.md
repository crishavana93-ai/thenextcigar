## Caption (paste)

No festival in Havana this year. So Habanos took its launches on tour.

The 26th Festival del Habano, planned for 23–27 February, was postponed and then cancelled on 20 July as Cuba's energy crisis went on. For years, that week was where Habanos announced its new cigars.

Instead, its headline 2026 launches went to five cities:
• Cannes, around 30 Sep: a new size for duty-free shops only, shown at a duty-free trade show.
• London, 7 Oct: a 60th-anniversary launch, about 400 guests.
• Madrid, 24 Oct: the year's first limited edition, premiered at the Las Ventas bullring.
• Macau, 16 Nov: a permanent size reserved for Habanos' La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist network, plus a humidor auction that raised US$753,750 for Cuba's public health system.
• Abidjan, 21 Nov: the first regional edition for the whole of Africa, rolling out from Côte d'Ivoire.

For 2027, no festival date has been announced. Habanos says it will return "when conditions allow".
