### Tobacco marketing (Lag 2018:2088 om tobak och liknande produkter)
No tobacco product, brand, image, price or purchase prompt appears on any slide. The only link is the account sign-off "THE NEXT CIGAR · thenextcigar.com", which is the publisher's name, not product marketing. **No breach found.** Low residual risk: keep the caption free of cigar products and links to shop or affiliate pages.

### Legal-advice check
Nothing reads as individual legal advice. Slide 2 states the rule more absolutely than the law does, which fixes 3–4 address. Slide 6 is consumer tips, not legal claims. "If it's missing, ask why" is fine. Do not strengthen it to "it's illegal" because of the exceptions.

## Exact text fixes (old → new)

1. **Slide 1, headline (required)**
   `Nearly 1 in 3<br>online shops<br><em>got their<br>discounts wrong.</em>`
   → `3 in 10<br>online shops checked<br><em>got their<br>discounts wrong.</em>`
   (If line length is a problem: `3 in 10<br>shops checked<br><em>got their<br>discounts wrong.</em>`)

2. **Slide 1, subline (recommended)**
   `An EU check of Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales.`
   → `An EU check of last year's Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales.`
   (If you prefer to stay strictly within the source wording: `An EU check of Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales, published March 2026.`)

3. **Slide 2, body (required)**
   `When a shop in the EU advertises a price reduction, it has to show the previous price. That previous price is the lowest one it charged in the 30 days before the cut.`
   → `When a shop in the EU advertises a price cut on goods, it has to show the previous price: the lowest it charged in the 30 days before the cut. A few exceptions apply, such as fresh food.`

4. **Slide 2, source line (recommended, future-proof)**
   `Sweden: prisinformationslagen 7 a §, in force since 1 Sep 2022.`
   → `Sweden: prisinformationslagen 7 a §, in force since 1 Sep 2022 (also covers services until 1 Mar 2027, if Prop. 2026/27:14 passes).`
   (Or keep it as is. It is accurate today. Keep "on goods" from fix 3 either way, because that is EU-accurate and stays Sweden-accurate after March 2027.)

5. **Slides 3 and 4, wording (recommended)**
   `online shops checked by consumer authorities` → `online sellers checked by consumer authorities`
   `showed their discounts incorrectly.` → `referenced their discounts incorrectly.` (optional; current wording is acceptable)
   `Share of the 314 shops checked.` → `Share of the 314 online sellers checked.`

6. **Slide 4, 18% row (required)**
   `used countdown timers or "almost sold out" claims. Over half of those were misleading.`
   → `used pressure tactics, like countdown timers or "running out" claims. Over half of those were misleading.`

7. **Slide 5, body (optional; reduces implied causation)**
   `Prisjakt checked about 500,000 products in October: just under 30% went up in price, and over 14% rose by at least 10%.`
   → `Prisjakt tracked about 500,000 products sold online in October: just under 30% went up in price, and over 14% rose by at least 10%.`

8. **Slide 6, tip 3 (required)**
   `Don't let a countdown timer rush you. The EU check found over half of those claims misleading.`
   → `Don't let a countdown timer rush you. The EU check found over half of the pressure tactics it saw were misleading.`

## Summary

Every number in the carousel matches its source exactly: 314, 23 EU countries plus Iceland and Norway, 30%, 36%, 18%, "over half", 10%, about 500,000, just under 30%, over 14%, 31 days, 1 Sep 2022 and 26 Mar 2026. None is wrong. Four places say more than the source does. (1) The hook drops "checked", so it reads as 30% of all online shops; "nearly 1 in 3" also rounds 30% up, where "3 in 10" is exact. (2) Slide 2 states the 30-day rule with no conditions. In EU law it covers goods only and has exceptions: perishables, newly launched products, stepped reductions, loyalty and personalised discounts. (3) and (4) Slides 4 and 6 present countdown timers and "almost sold out" claims, in quote marks, as the whole of the 18% category, but the Commission gives them only as examples of pressure selling. Swedish law currently covers services too. Prop. 2026/27:14 would limit 7 a § to goods from 1 Mar 2027, which does not make the current wording wrong, and adding "on goods" makes it safe on both sides of that date. There is no tobacco content and nothing that amounts to legal advice. With fixes 1, 3, 6 and 8 applied, the carousel is ready to publish.
