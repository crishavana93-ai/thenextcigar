## Caption (paste)

3 in 10 online shops checked in an EU sweep of Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales got their discounts wrong.

The rule: when a shop in the EU advertises a price cut on goods, it must show the lowest price it charged in the 30 days before.

The loophole: raise the price 31 days before, then "cut" it.
