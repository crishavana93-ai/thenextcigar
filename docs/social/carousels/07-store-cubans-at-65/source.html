The maker's own numbers · Habanos S.A.
65–70%relative humidity, at 16–18°C. That is Habanos' published range. Notice where it starts: not at 70.
The rule

The range starts at 65.

Every experienced Cuban smoker we know has drifted to the bottom of it, and the biggest Habanos forum online has spent twenty years converging on 62 to 65 in practice.

Why it matters

Cubans are rolled tighter.

A firm bunch and a thin wrapper take on water at 70% and swell. The draw closes, the burn wanders, and the flat wet-hay taste gets blamed on the cigar. It is the humidor.

Temperature · the part people skip
18°CHabanos' ceiling, and the reason is not flavour. It is beetles. Every cigar carries tobacco-beetle eggs; they hatch from about 23°C. A warm humidor is an incubator.
Temperature

Fix the heat first, the humidity follows.

Cooler air holds less water: the humidifier that reads 70% at 24°C reads 65% at 18°C. Coolest room in the flat, away from the radiator and the window.

Holding 65% in a Swedish winter · five rules
01Two-way packs at 65, not sponges. Double the count from November to March.
02Season a new box two weeks with distilled water before a single cigar goes in.
03Keep the dress boxes closed inside the humidor. Two layers of cedar, two buffers.
04Open the lid less. Every opening in January swaps 65% air for 25% air.
05A probe hygrometer in the drawer, never the gauge glued to the lid.
The method

Heating on, desert air.

A flat with the radiators on sits at 20–30% humidity. A wooden humidor in that room loses water through its walls every hour. This is the part the American guides never cover.

Calibrate the gauge · the salt test
75%A capful of table salt, a few drops of water, sealed in a bag with the hygrometer for eight hours. It must read 75. Whatever it reads instead is your offset, for good.
Trust nothing

The lid gauge is lying to you.

Two or three points off is normal; ten is not rare. The number that matters is inside the box where the cigars are, and only a probe reads it.

Dry cigars · bringing them back
Slowly.Two weeks at around 60%, then two at 65%, in a closed box with a pack. Straight into 70% and the leaf swells faster than the wrapper can stretch — it bursts. Weeks dry: recoverable. A year dry: the oils are gone.
