## Caption (paste)

Humidor dropping since the heating came on? It's probably not broken.

Winter air indoors is often dry. In a study of 678 Swedish homes over the 2007–08 heating season, about 37% fell in the two driest groups, and most readings in the driest group were below 20% humidity. A wooden humidor isn't sealed tight, so it trades moisture with that room. Boveda says a porous wooden box can sit 2–5 percentage points below its humidity pack, and calls glass tops "drafty".

The fixes, in order:
1. Test the hygrometer first: the salt test from our 11 November post. Boveda recommends recalibrating every six months.
2. Use a pack one step higher: 72% if you want 69%, 75% if you want 72% or have a glass top.
3. Worn seal? Double the packs.
4. Very dry room? Move to an airtight container, such as a sealed humidor bag or a tupperdor (a sealed plastic box).

Save this for when your reading drops. More on humidity at thenextcigar.com, link in bio. We don't sell tobacco.
