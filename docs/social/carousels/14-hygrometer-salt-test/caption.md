## Caption (paste)

Your humidor is only as right as the number on its hygrometer. And hygrometers drift: Boveda says even a freshly calibrated one is only accurate to about ±3–5%.

The fix costs nothing. Damp table salt sealed in a bag holds the air at 75% humidity, a figure published by the US National Bureau of Standards (now NIST). Put your hygrometer in with it overnight, read it through the bag, and you know exactly how far off it is.
