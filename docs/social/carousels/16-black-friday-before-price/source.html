The rule in Sweden · since 2022
30 daysA shop that advertises a price cut must also show the earlier price: the lowest price it charged in the 30 days before the cut.
The law

"Before" has a definition.

Prisinformationslagen, section 7 a, which comes from the EU's price indication directive.

The Next Cigar · source: SFS 2004:347, 7 a § · photo: Shixart1985, CC BY 2.03 / 7
The loophole · timing
31 daysA price raised 31 days before Black Friday is outside the 30-day window, Konsumentverket's lawyer told TT. By then, the raised price can be the "before" price.
Watch out

Look back further than 30 days.

Prisjakt's advice: check a product's price history over more than a month.

Shopping on Amazon.com? · read the label
List PriceThe suggested price from the maker or seller. Amazon shows it only if customers bought it on Amazon, or another retailer offered it, at or above that price in the past 90 days.
Typical PriceThe median price customers paid on Amazon over the past 90 days.
Read the label

Read the label next to the price.

A cut from a suggested price is not the same as a cut from what people usually pay.
