## Caption (paste)

Black Friday is on 27 November. Before you buy, check the "before" price.

Of about half a million products Prisjakt tracked in online shops, just under 30% rose in price during October.

The rule: since 2022, a shop that advertises a price cut in Sweden must also show the lowest price it charged in the 30 days before the cut.

The loophole: a price raised 31 days before Black Friday is outside that window, Konsumentverket's lawyer told TT. So look at the price history for longer than a month.

Konsumentverket's checks in 2025 found that 39% of the companies it reviewed broke the price information law, often with wrong reference prices.

Shopping on Amazon.com? Read the label next to the crossed-out price. "List Price" is a suggested price, which Amazon shows only if customers bought the product on Amazon, or another retailer offered it, at or above that price in the past 90 days. "Typical Price" is the median price customers paid on Amazon over 90 days.

Four checks: what the "before" price is, the price history beyond 30 days, a price-comparison site, and who is selling it.
