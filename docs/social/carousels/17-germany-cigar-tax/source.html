Cigar news · Germany

Germany will double its cigar tax.

The Bundestag passed the law on 15 October. The first rise comes on 1 March 2027, then every January until 2030 →

The Next Cigar · photo: Christian David, CC BY-SA 4.01 / 7
Today · tax on each cigar or cigarillo
1.4 centsplus 1.47% of the retail price. The minimum is 7.504 cents a piece, less the VAT in the retail price.
How it works

A fixed part, plus a share of the price.

Every cigar and cigarillo pays both parts. If they add up to less than the minimum, the minimum applies.

The steps

Four rises in four years.

The fixed part doubles by 2030. The share of the price jumps from 1.47% to 3.83% in March 2027.

The minimum · per cigar or cigarillo
7.5 → 14cents. The minimum rises from 7.504 today to 10.059 in March 2027, 11.258 in 2028, 12.488 in 2029 and 13.964 from 2030 (each less the VAT in the retail price).
The floor

The minimum nearly doubles.

13.964 cents is 1.86 times today's 7.504.

The Next Cigar · source: TabStG § 2 · photo: Karl Döringer, CC04 / 7
What cigars avoided · the late amendment
21.05%A late coalition amendment would have raised the price-based part for cigars from 1.47% to 21.05%, the cigar makers' association said. By its count, the tax could have risen up to 20-fold.
The reprieve

The final law stopped at 3.83%.

Cigars also got a later start: 1 March 2027, not 1 January. But the minimum per piece ended up higher than in the government's draft.

The market · Germany
2.1 billioncigars and cigarillos were taxed in Germany in 2025, down from 6.5 billion in 2007, according to the cigar makers' association. It counts about 1,600 employees under collective agreements.
Context

A third of 2007's volume.

The association's members are mostly medium-sized family firms, it says.
