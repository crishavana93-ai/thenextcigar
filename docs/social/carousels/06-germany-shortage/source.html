Why · the distributor
4 Sep5th Avenue, the sole Habanos distributor for Germany, Austria and Poland, tells retailers all orders and shipments are suspended, citing a sanctions review.
9 SepThe court in Waldshut-Tiengen orders preliminary insolvency administration. Retailers may keep selling stock on hand.
Behind it5th Avenue is 80% owned by Altabana, Habanos' own vehicle. Half of Habanos sits with Chen Zhi's Allied Cigar Corp. The EU sanctioned Chen Zhi and Prince Group on 30 July. A distributor that can't bank can't import.
The cause

One company. Three countries.

Retailers may sell what they have. What they have, on the board, is nearly nothing. Nobody has named a date for the next pallet.

The bill

The shortage premium.

Customs 26%, tobacco excise, then 19% VAT on all of it. Not a Habanos price rise — the cost of a box that has to cross a border.

What we'd do
Don'tpanic-buy. Every distribution crisis ends; nobody knows if this one ends in November or spring.
Doset a price alert on the vitolas you smoke. Free. One email when a German shop lists the box again, or a Swiss price falls under your number.
Readthe landed column before the sticker. CHF 420 that lands at €619 beats CHF 460 that lands at €678.
Refusethe box in your DMs at August prices. Shortages breed fakes. If they can't show the code, the seal and the receipt, no.
