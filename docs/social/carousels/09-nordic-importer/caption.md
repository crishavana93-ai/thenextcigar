## Caption

For thirty years, almost every legal box of Cuban cigars sold in Sweden came through one company in Göteborg. That company is now being wound up.

Elite Trading Scandinavia, known until recently as Habanos Nordic, imported for eight countries: Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Iceland and the three Baltic states. The City of Göteborg withdrew its tobacco licence last December, the courts upheld that, and the owners voted to liquidate on 1 July. As of today, no successor has been named.
