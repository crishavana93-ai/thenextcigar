Sweden has lost its Cuban cigar importer.

The company that brought in almost every legal Habanos box for the Nordics and the Baltics is being wound up. No successor has been named. Swipe for what happened →

The company

One importer. Eight countries.

Known until recently as Habanos Nordic AB. Flamman reported 87 million kronor in revenue for 2024, and Cigarrklubben estimated it handled about 45% of the Swedish cigar market.

What happened · the licence
15 Dec 2025The City of Göteborg withdraws the company's tobacco licence.
Summer 2026The administrative court rejects the company's appeal.
1 Jul 2026The shareholders vote for voluntary liquidation.
11 Sep 2026The last appeal is dismissed. The decision is final.
The cause

A licence lost. An appeal dropped.

Göteborg said the company didn't report changes to its owners and board, or send documents it asked for. It cited US charges against Chen Zhi, a part-owner above Habanos S.A. He has not been convicted.
