## Caption (paste)

Flying home for the holidays with a lighter? Check the rules before you pack.

• Flying to, from or within the US: torch lighters, the ones with a thin, needle-like jet flame, are banned in the cabin and in checked bags.
• Flying from Sweden: a disposable gas lighter or a small box of matches must be carried on your body. Lighters and matches are not allowed in hand luggage or checked bags.
• Lighter fuel and refills are not allowed.
• Electric lighters (US rules): carry-on only, no charging on board, and protect the switch so it can't turn on by accident.
• Cigar cutters: TSA says they're generally permitted but recommends packing them in checked baggage. The EU's cabin ban list names knives and scissors with blades over 6 cm; the list isn't exhaustive, so security staff can still refuse sharp items.

Airlines and other countries can add their own rules, so check with yours before you fly.
