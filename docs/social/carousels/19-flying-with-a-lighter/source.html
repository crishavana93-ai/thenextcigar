To, from or within the US · FAA and TSA
0 bagsTorch lighters, the ones with a thin, needle-like jet flame, are banned in the cabin and in checked baggage.
Torch lighters

Not in your carry-on, not in your suitcase.

TSA says the flame can reach 2,500°F, about 1,370°C.

Flying from Sweden · Transportstyrelsen
1 pocketA disposable gas lighter, or a small box of matches, must be carried on your body.
The rule

On you, never in a bag.

Lighters and matches are not allowed in hand luggage or in checked bags.

The Next Cigar · source: Transportstyrelsen · photo: Jeena Paradies, CC BY 2.03 / 7
Lighter fuel · all bags
0 refillsLighter fuel and refills are not allowed on you, in hand luggage or in checked bags.
Leave at home

The gas stays on the ground.

Sweden's Transportstyrelsen and IATA's rules for airlines both say so.

Electric lighters · US rules
WhereCarry-on only, never in checked bags.
ChargingNot allowed on board.
SwitchMust be protected so it can't turn on by accident.
Rechargeable

Cabin only, no charging.

Plasma, arc and coil lighters run on lithium batteries, so battery rules apply too.

Cigar cutters · EU and US
EUThe cabin ban list names knives and scissors with blades over 6 cm. Cutters are not named, but the list isn't exhaustive. Security staff can still refuse sharp items.
USTSA: generally permitted, but it recommends packing them in checked baggage.
Sharp things

TSA's advice: check it in.

At the checkpoint, TSA officers can stop any item they see as a threat.
