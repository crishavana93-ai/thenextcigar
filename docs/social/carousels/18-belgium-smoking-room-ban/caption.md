## Caption (paste)

Belgium's new smoking ban is on hold.

In September 2025, Belgium's federal government decided to ban public smoking rooms, "such as cigar clubs or shisha bars", including those in airports and hospitality, plus smoking and vaping on and near café terraces, from 1 January 2027.

That date won't hold. On 26 October, the office of health minister Frank Vandenbroucke said the Council of State, which reviews draft laws, "has asked us to amend certain measures, and we are currently working on this." No new date has been announced; parliament still has to pass the amended text. Reports on the delay name the terrace ban; the smoking-room ban is in the same draft law, so it can't take effect until that law passes.

It's the second delay: the terrace ban was first planned for 1 January 2026.

Already in force: since 1 April 2025, no tobacco, cigars included, may be displayed at the point of sale. Since 1 June 2026, cigars and cigarillos come under standardised packaging (shops may sell old stock until 1 January 2027).

We'll report the new date. More cigar news: link in bio. News, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.
