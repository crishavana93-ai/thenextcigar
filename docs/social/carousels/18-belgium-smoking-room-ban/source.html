Cigar news · Belgium

Belgium's new smoking ban is on hold.

One draft law would ban smoking on café terraces and close smoking rooms, cigar clubs included, from 1 January 2027. That date won't hold →

The Next Cigar · photo: Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.01 / 7
The plan · September 2025
1 Jan 2027Belgium's federal government decided to ban public smoking rooms, "such as cigar clubs or shisha bars", including those in airports and hospitality, plus smoking and vaping on and near terraces.
What was decided

Rooms, clubs and terraces.

The target: a smoke-free generation by 2040, the government says.

The Next Cigar · sources: Belga; VRT · photo: Steve Knight, CC BY 2.02 / 7
Already delayed once · terraces
2026 → 2027The terrace ban was first planned for 1 January 2026. It was pushed back a year so that everyone could prepare.
The history

A second delay.

On 26 October 2026, the health minister's office confirmed the 2027 date would not hold either.

The Next Cigar · source: Brussels Times · photo: S. Aperghis-Tramoni, CC BY-SA 2.03 / 7
Why · the Council of State
"Amend certain measures.""The Council of State has asked us to amend certain measures, and we are currently working on this," the office of health minister Frank Vandenbroucke said.
The review

The draft law goes back.

The Council of State reviews draft laws before they take effect. The minister's office has not said what changes it asked for.

The Next Cigar · sources: VRT; Brussels Times · photo: Trougnouf, CC BY 4.04 / 7
What's next · no new date
01The government amends the draft law.
02Parliament has to pass it.
03Venues get time to prepare, including the signs they must put up.
The timeline

No date announced.

Timing depends on how fast parliament passes the amended measures, VRT reports.
