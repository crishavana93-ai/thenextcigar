## Caption

Turn a Habanos box over and there are two lines of ink. One is the date the box was filled — three letters of Spanish, two digits of year, ABR 24 is April 2024 — and once you know the twelve stamps you can read it at any counter in ten seconds. The other is the factory code, and here is the thing nobody selling you a "current factory code table" will admit: Habanos has rotated it on a secret schedule since 2003, and the tables online are the 1985–1998 list with a new heading. Swipe for the month table, what the date actually means for ageing (filled, not harvested — the leaf is two years older), the cipher on vintage boxes, and why a familiar stamp still doesn't prove a box is real.
