## Caption

Turn a Habanos box over and there are two lines of ink. One is the date the box was filled: three letters of Spanish and two digits of year, so ABR 24 is April 2024. Once you know the twelve stamps, you can read it anywhere in seconds. The other line is the factory code, which Habanos has rotated on an unpublished schedule since about 2003. The tables online claiming to decode it are the 1985–1998 list under a new heading.
