The three charges · in the order they're added
Customs
duty		26% on cigars, the same across the EU.
Tobacco
excise		Set by each country, per cigar, as a percentage, or both.
VATYour country's rate, on the price plus the duty and excise. Sweden charges 25% on tobacco.
The three

Duty, excise, then VAT on top.

VAT is charged on the duty and excise as well as the price, which is why the total grows faster than you'd expect.

Travelling · cigars in your own luggage
Outside
the EU		50 cigars duty-free, or 100 cigarillos, or a proportionate mix. Above that, the three charges apply.
Within
the EU		Taxed in the country where you bought it. 200 cigars is a guide level for personal use, not a limit.
Travelling

In your luggage, different rules.

Countries can set higher guide levels than the EU's, but not lower. Check your own country's rules before you travel.

Ordering online · delivered to your door
Outside
the EU		Customs duty, excise and VAT are charged when it's imported.
Another
EU country		Excise is due in your country, and the seller must be registered there to pay it. Some countries ban it outright.
Online

By post, the border counts.

The duty-free allowances are for what you carry yourself. A parcel you've bought doesn't get them.
