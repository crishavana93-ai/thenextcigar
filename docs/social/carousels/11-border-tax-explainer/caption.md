## Caption

A box of cigars that crosses the EU border picks up three charges: 26% customs duty, your country's tobacco excise, then VAT on all of it, duty and excise included.

What you carry in your own luggage is treated differently from what arrives by post, and travelling inside the EU is different again. Swipe for the rules, with sources: the EU tariff, the excise directives and Tullverket.

## Sources
- EU tariff CN 2402 10 00: 26% (thetradehub.eu classification guide)
- Tullverket: VAT on tobacco is 25%; excise is paid to Customs on imports from outside the EU
- Directive 2008/118 Art. 32: travel within the EU, guide levels (minimum 200 cigars, 400 cigarillos)
- EVZ (European Consumer Centre Germany): from outside the EU, 50 cigars / 100 cigarillos duty-free
- Directive 2020/262 Art. 44: distance sales within the EU are taxed in the destination country; the seller is liable
