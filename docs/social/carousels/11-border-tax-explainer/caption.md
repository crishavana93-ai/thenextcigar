## Caption

A box of cigars that crosses the EU border can pick up three charges: customs duty (26% standard rate), your country's tobacco excise, then VAT on all of it, duty and excise included.

What you carry in your own luggage is treated differently from what arrives by post, and travelling inside the EU is different again. Swipe for the rules, with sources: the EU tariff, the excise directives and Tullverket.

Our allowance calculator, for the EU, the UK, Switzerland and Norway, is at the link in bio. Not legal or tax advice: check your own country's rules. We don't sell tobacco.

## Sources
- EU TARIC CN 2402 10 00: 26% standard rate; 0% for CARIFORUM, Central America and Mexico; 9.1% under GSP
- Tullverket: VAT on tobacco is 25%; excise is paid to Customs on imports from outside the EU
- Directive 2020/262 Art. 32 (replaced 2008/118 in Feb 2023): travel within the EU, guide levels (minimum 200 cigars, 400 cigarillos)
- Tullverket traveller page: from outside the EU, 50 cigars / 100 cigarillos duty-free; 18+; Swedish residents only on a commercial flight, after 20+ hours away, or for tobacco bought in Åland or Norway
- Directive 2020/262 Art. 44: distance sales within the EU are taxed in the destination country; the seller is liable

## Double-check
Independent check on 11 Nov 2026 (fresh agent, TARIC, Tullverket, Tulli and the directives opened). Slides 01–05 matched source.html. Fixed before posting: 26% is the standard (third-country) rate, not universal (0% CARIFORUM/Central America/Mexico, 9.1% GSP); excise can also be per kilo; the 50-cigar allowance has age and trip conditions (Sweden 18+, residents need a flight or 20+ hours away; Finland none for short land/sea trips); Directive 2008/118 replaced by 2020/262 Art. 32; slide 5 headline "without paying more?" read as cost-saving advice and became "What are the limits when you travel?"; #thenextcigar dropped. Open for the site, not the post: the allowance calculator's Swedish tax per cigar (Tullverket shows 2.19 SEK, Skatteverket's 2026 rate is 1.83 SEK).
