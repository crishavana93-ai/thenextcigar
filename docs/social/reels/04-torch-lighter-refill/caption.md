## Caption (paste)

Your torch lighter may not be broken. It may just have air trapped in the tank.

1. Bleed it. Valve away from your face, press it with a small screwdriver until the hissing stops.
2. Fill it upside down. One firm press, about five seconds.
3. Wait two to four minutes before you light it. Check your maker's instructions.

Never do this near a flame, and do it in a well-ventilated room.

Send this to the friend whose lighter never works.
