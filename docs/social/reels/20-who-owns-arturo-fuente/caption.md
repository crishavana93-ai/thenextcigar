## Caption (paste)

Who owns Arturo Fuente? Fire after fire, four generations, and, according to the company, still the same family.

• 1912: Arturo Fuente, a 24-year-old Cuban immigrant, opens a cigar factory in West Tampa, Florida. By 1922 it has about 500 workers.
• 1924: the factory burns while he is in Cuba buying tobacco. The company is out of business for 22 years. Cigar Aficionado counted eight fires in the company's history by 2012, and a tobacco warehouse in the Dominican Republic burned in May 2023.
• In the 1950s his son Carlos takes over the business, for one dollar.
• In the 1970s production moves to Nicaragua. That factory burns during the revolution (1978–79).
• 1980: the family starts again in Santiago, in the Dominican Republic.
• The industry said quality wrapper leaf couldn't be grown there. In 1991 the family planted it anyway, near Bonao.
• Today, the company says, it is still family-owned. It is run by Carlos Fuente Jr. Since 1986 it has worked with another family firm, J.C. Newman. In 2001 the two families set up a charity that runs a school and a health centre in the Dominican Republic.
