## Episode 1 (paste)

Why do some famous cigar names exist twice? Part 1 of 5.

In 1960, Cuba's new government seized the five leading cigar makers. Most of their owners fled.
• Alonso Menéndez and Pepe García (Montecristo, H. Upmann) started again in the Canary Islands.
• Fernando Palicio (Hoyo de Monterrey, Punch) reached the US and sold his names to a Tampa company. By 1965 the first of them was being made in Honduras.
• In 1972 a New York court ruled that the owners of five Cuban firms, most of them in exile, had not lost their US trademarks.
• From 1977, Ramón Cifuentes oversaw one of his old names, Partagás, being made again, in Jamaica.

So the same names came to exist in two versions. One is from Cuba, kept out of the US by the embargo. The other is from the Dominican Republic, Honduras or Nicaragua, and is the one found in the US (as of 2018).

The twist: Cohiba was created in Cuba in 1966, so it was never seized from a private owner. A US company registered the same name in the US in 1981. A US trademark board ordered that registration cancelled in 2022, and a federal court upheld the decision in 2025. The company is appealing.

## Episode 2 (paste)

Twin Names, part 2: the families who left.

• September 1960: soldiers enter Ramón Cifuentes' Partagás factory in Havana. "They asked me to stay," he told Cigar Aficionado in 1991. "I said no." He left for the US days later and went to work for the head of a big American tobacco company. After ten years of offers, his family made a deal in 1975. In 1977 Partagás came back, made in Jamaica.
• In 1935 Alonso Menéndez created Montecristo in Havana. A year later Pepe García joined him. In exile they started again in the Canary Islands, with a spin-off called Montecruz.
• Fernando Palicio ended up in Florida. At a meeting in Hialeah he made a deal on Hoyo de Monterrey and Punch. In all, he sold his names for almost $700,000.
• In 1968 the Trinidad family found a factory in Tampa to make their cigars. In 2001 they won the US trademark case over the Trinidad name.
