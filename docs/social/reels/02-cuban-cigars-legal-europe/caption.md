## Caption (paste)

Are Cuban cigars illegal in Europe? No. The rules people quote are American.

1. The US embargo is US law. The EU's Blocking Statute (Regulation 2271/96) makes US rulings based on it unenforceable in the EU and bars EU persons from complying with it.
2. US citizens may buy Cuban-origin goods in a third country for personal use there. Since 24 Sep 2020 they may not bring them into the US.
3. In Spain, France and Italy, tobacco is sold only through the state's licensed network. Spain and France ban online sales to consumers.
4. From 1 Jan 2027, it is an offence in the UK to sell tobacco to anyone born on or after 1 Jan 2009. In 2050, the youngest person who could be sold tobacco there would be 41.
