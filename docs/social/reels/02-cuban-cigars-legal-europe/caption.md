## Caption (paste)

Are Cuban cigars illegal in Europe? No. The rules people quote are American.

1. The US embargo is US law. The EU's Blocking Statute (Regulation 2271/96) makes US rulings based on it unenforceable in the EU and bars people and companies in the EU from complying with it, unless authorised.
2. US rules allow Cuban-origin tobacco for personal use outside the US. Since 24 Sep 2020 there is no exemption for travellers: no one, US or not, may bring it into the US.
3. In Spain (except the Canary Islands), mainland France and Italy, tobacco is sold only through state-licensed outlets. Spain and France ban online sales to consumers.
4. From 1 Jan 2027, it is an offence anywhere in the UK to sell tobacco to anyone born on or after 1 Jan 2009. In 2050, the youngest person who could be sold tobacco there would be 41.

What are the rules where you live? Comment your country and we'll reply with the official source.

Information, not legal advice. Checked 6 Nov 2026. Full country-by-country guide: link in bio.
