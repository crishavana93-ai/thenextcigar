Twin Names, part 5: who owns the twin names today?

• Cuba's side: every Cuban Cohiba, Montecristo and Partagás is sold worldwide through Habanos S.A. Half belongs to the Cuban state. The other half has had three private owners since 2000: Altadis (Spanish-French), then Imperial Tobacco (UK) from 2008, then Allied Cigar from 2020.
• Allied's biggest owner, through companies he owns, is Chen Zhi (57.1%), founder of Cambodia's Prince Group. In October 2025 the US and the UK sanctioned him and US prosecutors charged him; the EU followed in July 2026. He has been in custody in China since January 2026. He has denied wrongdoing, and the cigar companies have not been accused of any crime. In October 2026 a court in the British Virgin Islands ruled for him over control of his holding companies there; that case isn't over.
• America's side: Altadis USA sells the US Montecristo, H. Upmann and Romeo y Julieta. Scandinavian Tobacco Group (Denmark) owns the US Partagás, Hoyo de Monterrey and Punch, sold through its General Cigar and Forged divisions, and also sells the American Cohiba. A US trademark board and a federal court have ruled to cancel General Cigar's Cohiba trademark registrations; General Cigar is appealing.
• The twist: from about 2000 to 2020, one group owned half of Habanos and the US Montecristo too.
• In 2020 it was sold in two deals, to holding companies whose owners weren't disclosed. halfwheel reported the same investors were said to be behind both, and that the American side has since been sold on.
• Today, halfwheel reports that Allied's other owner has ties to the firm that owns the American side.

66 years after the seizures, the names are still split. And the story isn't over.

That's Twin Names, parts 1 to 5. History, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.

Sources: Habanos S.A.; Cigar Aficionado (27 Apr 2020); US Treasury and US DOJ (14 Oct 2025); OCCRP (Jul 2026); AP (8 Jan 2026); halfwheel (2025–26); Tobacco Reporter (Jan 2026); E.D. Va. (7 May 2025).
