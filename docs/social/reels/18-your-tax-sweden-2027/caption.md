## Caption (paste)

Sweden's cigar tax changes on 1 January, by one öre.

• 2026: 1.83 kr tax per cigar. 2027: 1.84 kr. On a box of 25, that's 45.75 kr in tax this year and 46.00 kr next year (tax only).
• That's the yearly inflation adjustment under the tobacco tax act, set by government ordinance in November 2026 (SFS 2026:1863).
• The hike that didn't happen: a Finance Ministry memo (Nov 2025) proposed an extra 3.5% on cigars, cigarettes, smoking tobacco and other tobacco. The memo put cigars at 1.89 kr. The 2027 budget bill dropped it, without saying why.
• At EU level, new minimum cigar taxes are still only a proposal.
