Sweden's cigar tax changes on January first. By one öre.
In 2026, the tax is one krona eighty-three per cigar. From 2027, it's one eighty-four. On a box of twenty-five, that's forty-five seventy-five in tax today, and forty-six kronor next year.
That's just the yearly inflation adjustment, fixed by government ordinance this November.
The bigger story is what didn't happen. A Finance Ministry memo proposed an extra three and a half percent on cigars, cigarettes and smoking tobacco. The memo put that at one eighty-nine per cigar. The 2027 budget bill dropped it, without saying why.
And at EU level, new minimum taxes on cigars are still only a proposal.
