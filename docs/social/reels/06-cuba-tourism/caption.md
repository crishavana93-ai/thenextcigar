## Caption (paste)

Cuba has lost almost two-thirds of its visitors this year.

From January to September 2026, 477,707 international visitors arrived, against 1,367,847 in the same months of 2025: 890,140 fewer, a fall of 65.1%. September, with 27,354, was the lowest month of the year so far. Canada, the biggest market, is down 76.9%.

Reports point to fuel shortages and blackouts, airlines cutting routes, US sanctions and foreign hotel groups leaving.

Why some headlines said 35%: this year's visitors are 34.9% of last year's. That's what remained, not what was lost.

Where do you think Cuba's tourism goes from here? Tell us in the comments.
