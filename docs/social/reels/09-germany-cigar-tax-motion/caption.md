## Caption (paste)

Germany will more than double its tax on cigars.

Today each cigar or cigarillo pays 1.4 cents plus 1.47% of its retail price, or a minimum if that is higher. A new law passed on 15 October changes that:
• From 1 March 2027: 2.14 cents plus 3.83%.
• Then every January, up to 2.85 cents in 2030. The fixed part doubles.
• The minimum nearly doubles, from 7.504 to 13.964 cents (less the VAT in the price).

The German cigar makers' association says 2.1 billion cigars and cigarillos were taxed in Germany in 2025, a third of the 2007 figure.
