## Caption (paste)

Germany will double its tax on cigars.

Today each cigar and cigarillo pays 1.4 cents plus 1.47% of its price. The Bundestag passed a new law on 15 October:
• From 1 March 2027: 2.14 cents plus 3.83%.
• Then every January, up to 2.85 cents in 2030. Double today.
• The minimum per cigar goes from 7.504 to 13.964 cents (less the VAT in the price).

Cigars escaped worse. According to the German cigar makers' association, an early draft of the coalition's amendment would have raised the price share to 21.05%. The amendment as passed kept 3.83%, but raised the minimum above the government's draft (13.964 cents instead of 9.69).

The association says Germany taxed 2.1 billion cigars and cigarillos in 2025, a third of the 2007 figure.

Full story, with every source: link in bio. News, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.
