Who owns Davidoff? The cigars belong to a Basel family company, Oettinger Davidoff, owned by the Schneider family. It reported 545.3 million Swiss francs in sales for 2025. In January 2026, Ernst Schneider's daughters handed the entire share capital to the next generation.
But the name itself is split. In 2006, Imperial Tobacco, now Imperial Brands, paid 540 million euros for the worldwide Davidoff cigarette trademark. Davidoff fragrances are made by Coty, under licence. Davidoff coffee is sold by Tchibo.
It started with one man. Zino Davidoff, born in 1906 in what is now Ukraine, grew up around his father's tobacco shop in Geneva. In 1970, he sold the business to Oettinger, run by Ernst Schneider. In 1989 he broke with Cuba, reportedly burning around 130,000 cigars. Production moved to the Dominican Republic.
