Who owns Davidoff? It depends on what you mean by Davidoff.

• The cigars: Oettinger Davidoff AG, a Basel family company owned by the Schneider family. 2025 sales were CHF 545.3 million. In January 2026, Ernst Schneider's daughters handed the entire share capital to the next generation.
• The name is split. In 2006, Imperial Tobacco (now Imperial Brands) paid €540 million for the worldwide Davidoff cigarette trademark. Davidoff fragrances are made by Coty under licence. Davidoff coffee is sold by Tchibo.
• It started with one man. Zino Davidoff, born in 1906 in what is now Ukraine, grew up around his father's tobacco shop in Geneva. In 1970 he sold the business to Oettinger, run by Ernst Schneider. In 1989 he broke with Cuba, reportedly burning around 130,000 cigars. By 1991, production had moved to the Dominican Republic.

One founder. Several owners. Business history, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.
