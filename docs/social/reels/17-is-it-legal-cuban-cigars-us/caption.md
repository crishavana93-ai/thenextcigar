## Caption (paste)

Can you bring Cuban cigars into the US? No.

• Not bought in Havana, not from duty-free, not from a shop in Europe. Under the US embargo rules (31 CFR Part 515), Cuban-origin tobacco and alcohol can't be imported for personal use. That has been the rule since 24 September 2020.
• For a few years it was different. From January 2015, authorised travellers to Cuba could bring back up to $100 of alcohol and tobacco, within a $400 limit. In October 2016 that cap was removed, and normal customs limits applied. In 2020 the door closed again.
• US rules do allow Americans to buy them abroad, but only for use abroad. They can't bring them home. Gifts by mail are excluded too.
• The new Cuba sanctions rules of September 2026 didn't change this.

Information, not legal advice. We don't sell tobacco.

Sources: 31 CFR §515.204, §515.560(c)(3), §515.585(c)–(d), §515.544 (eCFR, Nov 2026); OFAC Cuba FAQ 720; Federal Register 80 FR 2291 (16 Jan 2015), 81 FR 71372 (17 Oct 2016), 85 FR 60068 (24 Sep 2020); CACR amendments (30 Sep 2026).
