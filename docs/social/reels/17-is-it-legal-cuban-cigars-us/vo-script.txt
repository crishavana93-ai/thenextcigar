Can you bring Cuban cigars into the US? Short answer: no. Not bought in Havana, not from a duty-free shop, not from a store in Europe.
Under the US embargo rules, Cuban-origin tobacco and alcohol can't be imported for personal use. That has been the rule since September 2020.
Here's the surprise: for a few years it was allowed. In 2015, authorised travellers to Cuba could bring back up to one hundred dollars' worth, inside a four-hundred-dollar limit. From October 2016, that cap was gone. Normal customs limits applied. Then, in 2020, that door closed again.
US rules do allow Americans to buy them abroad, but only for use abroad. They can't bring them home.
Mailing them as a gift is excluded too. And the new Cuba sanctions rules of September 2026 didn't change any of this.
