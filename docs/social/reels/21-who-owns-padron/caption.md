## Caption (paste)

Who owns Padrón? A hammer, three countries and one family.

• José Orlando Padrón was born in Cuba in 1926. He left in 1961 and reached Miami in 1962, living on $60 a month in refugee aid.
• According to the company, a friend gave him a small hammer. He gardened by day, did carpentry by night, and saved $600.
• September 1964: he opens a cigar company in Miami, with one roller. He later also opens a factory in Estelí, Nicaragua. In May 1978 the factory and warehouse there burned.
• Autumn 1978: he goes to Havana with a group of Cuban exiles for talks with Castro's government about political prisoners.
• In 1979 and 1980 Omega 7, a militant anti-Castro exile group, claimed bombings of his Miami factory. Reports at the time, in the Miami Herald and New York magazine, noted his part in the Havana talks. The company's own biography blames "a small group of local extortionists".
• 1985: a US embargo bans all imports from Nicaragua. For a time in the 1980s the company rolled its cigars in Honduras.
• He died in 2017, aged 91. Today the company is still run by his family, led by his son Jorge.

Business history, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.
