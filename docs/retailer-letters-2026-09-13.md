# Retailer letters — a labelled featured slot

**13 September 2026.** Five retailers, one offer, one template. Send from hello@thenextcigar.com, plain text, no attachments, subject line as given.

## What you are selling, exactly

A clearly-labelled **Featured retailer** slot — "Featured · paid placement" in the same small type the paper uses for everything else — in two places:

1. On the country page of the Finder for their country (`/finder/de/`, `/finder/ch/`, `/finder/it/`), above the retailer list.
2. On the retailer-map article — your single most-read page, 297 clicks a quarter — in the section for their country.

What it is **not**: it never touches the price ranking. Their box prices sit in the board exactly where the numbers put them, same as everyone's. That neutrality is the thing they are buying access to, and it is the sentence to repeat if anyone asks for more.

## The number

Until migration 031 has been live a month you do not have a click figure per retailer, and the letters below are written to work without one. When you have it, replace the bracketed line. Do not invent one in the meantime — a retailer who checks their own referrer logs and finds you rounded up is a retailer you never write to again.

## Price

Start low and short. The first customer is a reference, not revenue.

- **€120 a month, three-month minimum, paid up front** (€360). Cancel any time after.
- After three months, review against the click count and reprice honestly — up if it delivered, down or free if it did not.

Why that figure: your whole map page sees ~100 clicks a month across all retailers. A featured slot might send one retailer 20–40 of those. At a €300 box and a 30% margin, one order pays for the month. That is an easy yes for them and a number you can defend.

## Sequence

Noblego and EGM first, a day apart. Wait a week. Then the other three, using whichever of the first two said yes as the reference — "Noblego took the German slot" is a better opening line than anything I can write for you.

## The one thing not to do

Do not offer to move anyone up the board, add a star, hide a competitor, or soften a price comparison. The moment the board is for sale, the map page stops being worth advertising on. The slot is sold *around* the board, never *in* it.
