## Never stock — affiliate only

**Lighters and butane.** Butane is UN 1057, dangerous goods class 2.1. It cannot travel by ordinary air post, which means you cannot legally fulfil a lighter order from Sweden by post and a Chinese supplier cannot air-freight one to your customer either. This is not a preference, it is transport law.

**Three lighter listings are currently live on your shop** — the JOBON triple flame, the quad flame with V-cutter, and the triple jet torch. If somebody orders one this week you cannot post it. They need to come down, or turn into affiliate recommendations.

That is also the answer to the 106 clicks a quarter on the lighters guide: it is the affiliate page, permanently. It monetises through Amazon the day Associates approves the account, and it never holds stock.

## The programme, in order

1. **Take the three lighters off sale.** They are unfulfillable. Convert the guide to affiliate instead.
2. **Open the five Tier 1 suppliers.** One message each, the template in the supplier pack. Ask for the factory name and address every time — the EU responsible-person rule means you cannot list a product without it.
3. **Order samples, not stock.** Five cutters, two scissors, two cases, and a Boveda trade sample. Under SEK 900 across three suppliers.
4. **Build the pages while the parcels are in the air.** They go up with `comingSoon: true` and the supplier's photograph, captioned as the supplier's. Nothing is sellable yet.
5. **Handle every item when it lands.** Reject anything that cuts badly, feels hollow, or will wear through in a month. A bad cutter costs a reader, and readers are the expensive thing here.
6. **Photograph the survivors yourself** — on paper, in daylight, in the newspaper's own language. Then flip `comingSoon` off, one product at a time.
7. **Reorder at fifty.** The leather case is SEK 79 at two pieces and SEK 36 at fifty. The gap between the test price and the volume price is the entire business.

## Costs that have to stay in the arithmetic

Since 1 July 2026 the EU charges a flat **€3 customs duty per item** on consignments of €150 or under from outside the EU, running to July 2028. Import VAT is due regardless of value; Swedish VAT is 25%.

On a bulk order arriving as one consignment, €3 an item is survivable. On single parcels drop-shipped from China to each customer it is the whole margin. That is the arithmetic that decides drop-ship versus stock, product by product — and it is why Boveda and the cutters want to be stock, while the ashtray and the travel humidor can stay drop-ship until they prove themselves.
