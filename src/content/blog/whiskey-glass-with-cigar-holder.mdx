## What a cigar-holder glass is

It is an ordinary old fashioned (rocks) glass with a dent pressed into one side. A cigar lies in the dent, so one hand can hold both the glass and the cigar. That is the whole idea. Nothing about it changes the drink or the cigar.

## When it's worth it

- **You're standing.** At a party or on a patio with nowhere to put things down, one hand for both leaves the other free. This is the case the glass was made for.
- **There's no ashtray nearby.** The rest keeps a lit cigar off the table edge or the railing between puffs.
- **It's a gift for someone who has the basics.** It costs under $20, it is clearly for a cigar smoker, and it isn't one more cutter.

## When it's a gimmick

- **At a table.** If there is an ashtray within reach, the cigar belongs there. Ash falls where it should, and the smoke isn't right under your nose each time you lift the glass.
- **With big cigars.** The rest is one fixed size. None of the listings we read says how thick a cigar it takes, so if he smokes 60-ring Gordos, read the buyer reviews first or choose the tray.
- **If you want easy washing.** Godinger says its glass is handmade crystal and must be washed by hand. A dishwasher can crack the rim.
- **If the glass matters more than the trick.** People who care about their whisky usually already have a glass they like. A glass with a dent in it won't replace it.

## Questions people ask

**Is a whiskey glass with a cigar holder a good gift?** Yes, for a smoker who entertains or smokes outdoors, and at under $20 it's a low-risk one. For someone serious about whisky, give proper glasses.

**Does the cigar change the taste of the drink?** The glass keeps them apart, so nothing touches. The cigar does sit close to your nose when you drink, which some people like and some don't.

**Will any cigar fit?** The rest is a fixed size and the listings we read don't give its width. For very thick cigars, check the buyer reviews before you order.

**Can it go in the dishwasher?** Godinger says no: hand wash only. Jezyki's listing says its glasses are dishwasher safe. Check the listing for the one you buy.
