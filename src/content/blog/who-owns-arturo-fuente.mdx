*This is the full story behind our "Who owns it?" video on Arturo Fuente. Every fact below has a source at the end. Business history, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.*

## The short answer

According to the company, Arturo Fuente is **still family-owned and family-run**, four generations after it was founded. It is run by **Carlos Fuente Jr.**, the founder's grandson. There is no public share register, so the ownership claim rests on the company's own statement; we found no report of a sale or outside investor.

## Timeline

- **1912:** Arturo Fuente, a 24-year-old Cuban immigrant, opens a cigar company in West Tampa, Florida.
- **1922:** the company employs about 500 people.
- **1924:** the factory burns while Arturo is in Cuba buying tobacco. The company is out of business for 22 years.
- **1950s:** his son Carlos takes over the business for one dollar. (The company says 1956; Cigar Aficionado says 1958. Both say one dollar.)
- **1970s:** production moves to Nicaragua. That factory burns during the revolution (1978 according to the company, 1979 according to Cigar Aficionado).
- **1980:** the family opens a factory in Santiago, Dominican Republic.
- **1986:** the Fuente family begins its partnership with another family firm, J.C. Newman of Tampa.
- **1991:** the industry consensus was that quality wrapper leaf could not be grown in the Dominican Republic. The family planted it anyway, on land near Bonao.
- **2001:** the Fuente and Newman families set up the Cigar Family Charitable Foundation, which runs a school and a health centre in the Dominican Republic.
- **By 2012:** Cigar Aficionado counted eight fires in the company's history. A tobacco warehouse in the Dominican Republic burned again in May 2023.
- **2016:** Carlos Fuente Sr. dies in Tampa, aged 81.
- **Today:** Carlos Fuente Jr. is president of the company.

## Questions people ask

### Is Arturo Fuente still family-owned?

According to the company's own history page, yes: "still family owned and operated". There is no public share register to confirm it, and we found no report of a sale.

### Where was Arturo Fuente founded?

In West Tampa, Florida, in 1912. Production moved to Nicaragua in the 1970s and to Santiago, in the Dominican Republic, in 1980.

### What is the Fuente and Newman partnership?

Since 1986 the Fuente family has worked with J.C. Newman, another family cigar company from Tampa. In 2001 the two families set up a charity that runs a school and a health centre in the Dominican Republic.
