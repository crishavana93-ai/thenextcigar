**In short:** The **ProCigar Festival** will be held from **16 to 19 February 2027** in **Santiago de los Caballeros**, the centre of the Dominican Republic's cigar industry, according to Cigar Aficionado, halfwheel and Cigar Coop. Cigar Aficionado says it is the 19th edition. Like last year it runs four days, but halfwheel reports that the optional La Romana leg of earlier festivals is not offered. It is also the first festival announced since **Davidoff** and its Dominican company left ProCigar, the association of Dominican cigar makers that organises it, Cigar Coop reports.

*Based on reports by Cigar Aficionado, halfwheel and Cigar Coop, September 2026. Checked 6 November 2026.*

## What ProCigar is

ProCigar is an association of premium cigar manufacturers in the Dominican Republic. It usually holds its festival in February, when visitors tour member factories and tobacco farms around Santiago (in earlier years, also in La Romana). Cigar Coop describes it as one of the three major cigar festivals in the producing countries, alongside Cuba's Festival del Habano and Nicaragua's Puro Sabor.

## The 2027 dates and format

| | What we know |
|---|---|
| Edition | 19th (Cigar Aficionado) |
| Dates | Tuesday 16 to Friday 19 February 2027 |
| Place | Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic |
| Length | Four days |
| Organiser | ProCigar, the association of Dominican cigar manufacturers |

*Sources: Cigar Aficionado, 1 September 2026; halfwheel, 7 September 2026.*

According to halfwheel, the week is set out like this:

- **Tuesday:** a welcome event.
- **Wednesday to Friday, daytime:** tours of member factories and tobacco farms. New this year is a Wednesday "Hopping Tour" of visits to businesses that supply the factories.
- **Wednesday evening:** a traditional dinner.
- **Thursday evening:** the White Party at Santiago's monument, which Cigar Coop identifies as the Monument to the Heroes of the Restoration.
- **Friday evening:** a gala dinner and auction; Cigar Aficionado describes it as a charity auction.

Cigar Coop also lists a new Discovery Tour, a "Power Talk", a "Women's Talk" and a beach day among the 2027 activities.

## Davidoff has left ProCigar

Cigar Coop reports that **Davidoff** and its Dominican manufacturing company, **TABADOM Holdings**, are no longer members of ProCigar, which leaves the association with 11 active members. Halfwheel also reports that Davidoff has left, and its 2027 tour list includes no Davidoff factory. Neither outlet gives a reason for the departure.

## Cuba's festival

The other big festival in a producing country is Cuba's Festival del Habano in Havana, organised by Habanos S.A. rather than by an association of makers. Our guide to it is [here](/blog/your-guide-to-habanos-festival/).

## Questions

### When is the ProCigar Festival 2027?

From 16 to 19 February 2027, according to Cigar Aficionado and halfwheel.

### Where is the ProCigar Festival held?

In and around Santiago de los Caballeros, in the Dominican Republic. Earlier festivals also offered an optional leg in La Romana; halfwheel reports it is not part of the 2027 programme.

### Is Davidoff part of ProCigar Festival 2027?

No. Cigar Coop reports that Davidoff and TABADOM Holdings have left ProCigar, and halfwheel also reports the departure.
