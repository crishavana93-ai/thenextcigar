**In short:** Yes. Cuban cigars are legal in every EU country and in the UK. The ban most people have heard of is the **US embargo**, and it is American law. The EU has opposed it for decades, and its own Blocking Statute makes rulings based on it unenforceable in the EU. What does differ across Europe is everything around the cigar: **who is allowed to sell tobacco, the minimum age, how much you may carry across a border, and whether you may order it online.** Those rules apply to all cigars, Cuban or not.

*Checked against official sources on 4 November 2026. This is general information, not legal advice.*

## Why people think they are banned

The confusion comes from the United States. Its Cuban Assets Control Regulations stop Cuban-origin goods from entering the US, and that is the rule travellers and films repeat.

The EU's position is the opposite. In its statement on the 2022 UN General Assembly vote on the embargo, the EU said it has long opposed the US measures "due to their extraterritorial application" and their impact on the EU ([EEAS](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/un-new-york/eu-explanation-vote-un-general-assembly-resolution-embargo-imposed-usa-against-cuba_en)). Its Blocking Statute, Regulation 2271/96, lists the US Cuba laws by name. It makes court rulings based on them unenforceable in the EU (Art. 4) and bars EU persons from complying with them (Art. 5) ([Regulation 2271/96](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/1996/2271/pdfs/eur_19962271_adopted_en.pdf)).

So in Europe a Cuban cigar is treated like any other cigar: a tobacco product, taxed and age-restricted, sold through whatever channel each country allows.

## Country by country

| Country | Who may sell tobacco | Minimum age to buy | Online sales to consumers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Italy | State monopoly network of licensed *rivendite* (tabaccherie) | 18 | We have not yet confirmed the rule in an official source |
| Spain | Only the state network of *estancos* (expendedurías de tabaco y timbre); the Canary Islands have their own system | 18 | Banned |
| France | Only licensed *buralistes*, under the state monopoly run by customs | 18 | Buying tobacco online is banned, wherever the website is |
| Germany | Ordinary retailers; no state monopoly | 18 | Allowed for adults; mail order to minors is banned |
| Ireland | Registered retailers | 18 (a rise to 21 is planned) | See the cross-border rules below |
| Sweden | Retailers with a municipal sales permit | 18 | See the cross-border rules below |
| United Kingdom | Ordinary retailers | 18; no sales ever to anyone born on or after 1 Jan 2009, from 1 Jan 2027 | See the cross-border rules below |

### Italy

Tobacco in Italy is a state monopoly managed by the customs and monopolies agency (ADM). By law, "la vendita al pubblico di tabacchi lavorati è effettuata a mezzo di rivendite ovvero di patentini": retail sale happens only through licensed outlets, the *rivendite* most people know as tabaccherie, or holders of a special permit ([Decree 38/2013, Art. 1](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/index.php/files/3284/decreto-21022013-38.pdf)). ADM sets where those outlets may open ([ADM determination, 2 July 2021](https://www.adm.gov.it/portale/documents/20182/62932379/DETERMINA_R_U_231333.02-07-2021-U.pdf/b74057ef-a1ed-5a43-0806-1b48b8047952)). Selling tobacco to anyone under 18 is an offence ([Decree-Law 158/2012, Art. 7](https://www.adm.gov.it/portale/documents/20182/6661054/dl+n.+158+del+2012+art+7.pdf/39a0d9bc-0bf2-4856-b8b0-5c584d606f2a)).

So, can you buy Cuban cigars in Italy? Yes, legally, through that licensed network, if you are 18 or over.

### Spain

Spain's tobacco law reserves retail sale to the state network of *expendedurías de tabaco y timbre* (estancos) and authorised vending machines (Ley 28/2005, Art. 3.1). It bans selling tobacco to under-18s (Art. 3.2) and bans indirect or distance retail sales, which covers online and mail order (Art. 3.6) ([BOE, consolidated text](https://boe.es/buscar/pdf/2005/BOE-A-2005-21261-consolidado.pdf)). The network is run by the Comisionado para el Mercado de Tabacos; the Canary Islands sit outside it ([CMT guide for estancos](https://www.hacienda.gob.es/Documentacion/Publico/Tabacos/Expendedurias/GuiaParaExpendedores.pdf)).

### France

France has a state retail monopoly on tobacco, run by customs. *Buralistes* sell as agents of the administration ([French customs](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/fiche/monopole-de-la-vente-au-detail-des-tabacs-manufactures-les-textes-en-vigueur)). Buying tobacco remotely is banned "whatever the location of the website" (General Tax Code, Art. 568 ter) ([French customs: buying tobacco online](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/fiche/achat-de-tabac-et-de-cigarettes-sur-internet)). If you bring tobacco back from another EU country, you must be 18, and the indicative level is 200 cigars or 400 cigarillos ([French customs](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/actualites/quelle-quantite-de-tabac-ramener-depuis-le-luxembourg-lespagne-ou-litalie); [Service-Public.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F804)).

### Germany

Germany has no tobacco monopoly; cigars are sold by ordinary retailers. The Youth Protection Act bans handing tobacco to children or young people and bans mail-order sales to them ([JuSchG §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/juschg/__10.html)).

The legal position is not the problem in Germany this autumn; supply is. The official importer of Habanos stopped deliveries in September and then filed for insolvency. We covered what that did to German shelves in [Germany has run out of Cuban cigars](/blog/germany-cuban-cigar-shortage-september-2026/).

### Ireland

Ireland's minimum age is 18 ([Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002, s.45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/sec0045.html)). A bill to raise it to 21 was notified to the European Commission in 2024 ([TRIS 2024/0347/IE](https://technical-regulation-information-system.ec.europa.eu/en/notification/26010/print)). News reports say it is due to take effect on 1 February 2028; we have not yet seen that date in an official source.

Revenue's guide levels for tobacco brought from another EU country are 200 cigars or 400 cigarillos, and you must be 17 or over. Since 9 December 2025, Revenue says it can seize the whole quantity if you exceed them ([Revenue](https://www.revenue.ie/en/customs/individuals/info-travelling-duty-free/from-within-eu/duty-paid.aspx)).

### Sweden

Cuban cigars are legal in Sweden, sold by retailers that hold a municipal permit, to buyers aged 18 or over. As in Germany, the issue this year is supply: the importer for Sweden, the other Nordic countries and the Baltics lost its licence in September. The full story and what it means is in [Sweden's Cuban cigar importer is being wound up](/blog/sweden-cuban-cigar-importer-liquidation-2026/).

### United Kingdom

The UK left the EU, but it never applied the US embargo either. The minimum age is 18 ([SI 2007/767](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/cy/uksi/2007/767/made/data.pdf)). The Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 goes further: from **1 January 2027** it is an offence to sell a tobacco product to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009 ([s.1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/1/enacted); [s.175](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/175/enacted)). That age line never moves, so the group who can never legally be sold tobacco in the UK grows every year.

Arriving in Great Britain from abroad, the duty-free allowance is one of: 200 cigarettes, 100 cigarillos, **50 cigars** or 250 g of tobacco. You can split it (half the cigars and half the cigarettes, for example), and there is no allowance under 17 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/duty-free-goods/arriving-in-Great-Britain)).

## Americans in Europe

US citizens and residents may buy and smoke Cuban cigars while they are in Europe. The US Treasury says US persons "may purchase or acquire Cuban-origin merchandise, including alcohol and tobacco products, while in a third country for personal consumption outside the United States" ([OFAC FAQ 720](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/720)).

What they may not do is take them home. Since 24 September 2020, US rules no longer allow Cuban-origin alcohol or tobacco to be brought into the United States, even as personal baggage ([Federal Register, 85 FR 60068](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2020-09-24/html/2020-21084.htm); [OFAC FAQ 769](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/769)). The rule covers every traveller arriving in the US, Europeans included, not just US citizens.

## Carrying cigars across a border

**Between EU countries.** Tobacco you buy duty-paid for your own use and carry yourself is taxed where you bought it. EU law sets guide levels that help customs decide whether goods are for personal use: at least **200 cigars** or 400 cigarillos per person. Countries may set higher levels, not lower ones ([Directive 2020/262, Art. 32](https://legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2020/262/chapter/V/section/1/adopted/data.pdf)). These are guide levels, not hard limits, but going over them means you may have to show the goods are for your own use, and in Ireland the whole quantity can now be seized.

**Arriving in the EU from outside it,** from the UK or Switzerland for example, the duty-free allowance is **50 cigars** or 100 cigarillos. Countries may lower that to 10 cigars or 20 cigarillos for people who do not arrive by air ([Directive 2007/74, Art. 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2007/74/article/8)). Above the allowance you pay customs duty, excise and VAT in the country you enter.

**Ordering online from another country** is not covered by any of this. The excise of the country you live in is due on delivery, the seller has to be set up to pay it, and Spain and France ban distance sales to consumers outright. ## Questions

### Are Cuban cigars illegal in Europe?

No. No EU country and not the UK bans Cuban cigars. The US embargo is American law, and the EU's Blocking Statute bars EU persons from complying with it. Cuban cigars are taxed and age-restricted like any other tobacco product.

### Can you buy Cuban cigars in Italy?

Yes. Tobacco in Italy is sold through the state monopoly's licensed outlets (tabaccherie), and you must be 18 or over.

### Are cigars legal in the UK?

Yes, for anyone aged 18 or over. From 1 January 2027 it becomes an offence to sell tobacco, cigars included, to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009.

### Can Americans buy Cuban cigars in Europe?

Yes, for personal use while they are outside the United States. Since September 2020 they may not bring Cuban cigars back into the US.

### How many cigars can I bring home from another EU country?

The EU guide level is at least 200 cigars per person, for your own use, carried by you, with tax paid in the country where you bought them. Some countries set higher levels.

### Can I order Cuban cigars online from another EU country?

Only if the seller pays the excise due in your country, and not at all into Spain or France, which ban distance sales of tobacco to consumers.
