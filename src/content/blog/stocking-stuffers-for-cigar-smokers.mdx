## What to skip

- **Cigars.** Taste is personal, and a cigar bought without knowing what he smokes is a guess.
- **Novelty lighters.** A lighter shaped like something else is a joke for one evening. A plain torch gets used.
- **Sets with ten tools in one box.** They look generous, but you can't judge each tool from the listing. One good cutter is the safer present.

## Questions people ask

**What's a good cheap gift for a cigar smoker?** A cutter or a punch. Every smoker uses one every time, and they get lost. The punch and the cutter here.

**What do you put in a stocking for a cigar smoker?** Small things that get used up or lost: a punch, a humidity pack, a travel tube, a lighter.

**Is it OK to give cigars as a gift?** Only if you know exactly what he smokes. If you don't, give the things around the cigar.
