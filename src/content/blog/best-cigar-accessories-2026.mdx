## 1. Somewhere to keep them: a humidor

Cigars need stable humidity and temperature. Habanos S.A. recommends 16–18 °C and 65–70% relative humidity. A box with a good seal and a Spanish cedar lining is the starting point.

**The pick for a first humidor: [Tesonway glass-top humidor](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CP1ZNWJ7?tag=thenextcigar-20)** ($35.99, 4.4★ from 2,094 ratings). A cedar-lined desktop box with a glass lid, at the price of a few cigars.

**If you want it to last: [Case Elegance Renzo](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07GXSVH1H?tag=thenextcigar-20)** ($88.75, 4.6★ from 6,440 ratings). Sold with thick cedar, and rated by more than 6,000 Amazon buyers.

## 2. Something to hold the humidity: a Boveda pack

A humidor is only a box. What holds the humidity is the humidifier, and the easiest kind is a two-way pack: it releases moisture when the air is dry and absorbs it when it's damp.

**The pick: [Boveda 65%, size 60](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00FWPQ8WC?tag=thenextcigar-20)** ($8.50, 4.8★ from 1,464 ratings). ## 3. A way to check it: a hygrometer

You can't see humidity. A hygrometer tells you whether the pack is doing its job, and when it's time to replace it.

**The pick: [Govee H5075](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07Y36FWTT?tag=thenextcigar-20)** ($12.99, 4.5★ from 48,591 ratings). Govee rates it at ±3% RH, it shows the reading on your phone so you don't have to open the lid, and its app has a calibration setting.

Every hygrometer drifts, new ones included. Check yours with table salt or a Boveda 75% kit: **[how to calibrate a cigar hygrometer](/blog/how-to-calibrate-a-cigar-hygrometer/)**.

## Quick answers

**What accessories does a beginner need?** A humidor, a two-way humidity pack, a hygrometer, a cutter and a lighter. The starter kit above comes to $80.55 at today's Amazon prices.

**What humidity should a humidor be at?** Habanos S.A. says 65–70% at 16–18 °C. We keep Cuban cigars at 65%.

**Do I need a hygrometer if I use Boveda packs?** Yes. The pack holds the humidity; the hygrometer tells you when the pack is spent, or when the humidor isn't sealing.

**V-cut, straight cut or punch?** All three work. A straight cut suits most shapes, a V-cut takes a narrow wedge that concentrates the draw on thicker rings, and a punch works best on mid-size rings.
