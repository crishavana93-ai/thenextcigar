*This is the full story behind our "Who owns it?" video on Padrón. Every fact below has a source at the end. Business history, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.*

## The short answer

Padrón is **run by the Padrón family, led by Jorge Padrón**, son of the founder José Orlando Padrón. Cigar Aficionado describes it as family-owned. The share register is private, and we found no report of a sale or outside investor.

## Timeline

- **1926:** José Orlando Padrón is born in Cuba.
- **1961:** he leaves Cuba by plane, via Spain.
- **1962:** he reaches Miami, living on $60 a month in refugee aid.
- **The hammer:** according to the company, a friend gave him a small hammer. He worked as a gardener by day and a carpenter by night, and saved $600.
- **September 1964:** he opens a cigar company in Miami with one roller. He later also opens a factory in Estelí, Nicaragua.
- **May 1978:** the factory and warehouse in Estelí burn. (Sources disagree about who was responsible, so we don't name anyone.)
- **Autumn 1978:** he travels to Havana with a group of Cuban exiles for talks with Castro's government about political prisoners.
- **1979 and 1980:** Omega 7, a militant anti-Castro exile group, claims bombings of his Miami factory. Reports at the time, in the Miami Herald and New York magazine, noted his part in the Havana talks. The company's own biography blames "a small group of local extortionists".
- **After 1979:** Padrón later said he set up a makeshift factory in Honduras. According to Cigar Aficionado, Padrón cigars were rolled in Danlí, Honduras, in the 1980s; that factory closed in 2007.
- **1985:** a US executive order bans all imports from Nicaragua.
- **2017:** José Orlando Padrón dies, aged 91.
- **Today:** the company is run by his descendants, led by his son Jorge.

## Questions people ask

### Who owns Padrón today?

The Padrón family. Cigar Aficionado describes the company as family-owned and run by the founder's descendants, led by his son Jorge. The share register is private.

### When was Padrón founded?

In September 1964, in Miami, by José Orlando Padrón, with one roller.

### Why were Padrón cigars made in Honduras?

After the revolution in Nicaragua, Padrón set up a factory in Danlí, Honduras. In 1985 a US executive order banned all imports from Nicaragua. According to Cigar Aficionado, the cigars were rolled in Honduras in the 1980s, and that factory closed in 2007.
