*This is the full story behind our "Who owns it?" video on Davidoff. Every fact below has a source at the end. Business history, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.*

## The short answer

There is no single owner of "Davidoff". The name is split between several companies:

| What | Who owns or runs it |
|---|---|
| **Cigars** | Oettinger Davidoff AG, Basel, a family company owned by the Schneider family |
| **Cigarettes** | Imperial Brands (formerly Imperial Tobacco), which bought the worldwide cigarette trademark in 2006 |
| **Fragrances** | Made by Coty under licence |
| **Coffee** | Sold by Tchibo |

## The cigar company

Oettinger Davidoff AG is registered in Basel, Switzerland. The Swiss commercial register describes it as an economically independent family company. It sits alongside a holding company, Oettinger Davidoff Holding AG, also in Basel.

Its 2025 sales were **CHF 545.3 million**, up 2.5% in real terms, according to the company's own annual release of 1 July 2026. Within that, the Davidoff brand grew 2.4% and the company's second brand, Zino, grew 16.1%. The company says it made 36.6 million handmade cigars in 2025; halfwheel reports that this was down from 38.5 million the year before. Finews reported in 2025 that the group employs more than 4,000 people; about 160 of them work at the Basel head office, according to the company.

The company is run by CEO **Beat Hauenstein**, in the role since 2017, and its chairman is **Domenico Scala**.

## Who the owners are

The owners are the **Schneider family**. Dr Ernst Schneider, a Basel lawyer (1921–2009), took over the management of Oettinger in 1961; he was the son-in-law of the previous owner, Georg Huppuch. Schneider is the man who did the deal with Zino Davidoff and built the business that carries his name today.

In January 2026 Schneider's two daughters, Lilian Schaffner-Schneider and Christine Ryhiner-Schneider, transferred the **entire share capital** to their direct descendants. According to Tobacco Reporter, the management team did not change and the family keeps two of the six seats on the board. Chairman Domenico Scala called it "a milestone in our 150-year history".

One detail: in 2014 the then chairman, Andreas Schmid, became the company's first shareholder from outside the family, while the family kept a majority (Moodie Davitt Report). No source we found says whether that stake still exists, so we describe the company as **family-owned**, not 100% family-owned.

## Why the name is split

The Davidoff name has been licensed and sold in pieces over the decades.

- **Cigarettes.** In 2002 Imperial Tobacco bought the German group Reemtsma and with it the job of making Davidoff cigarettes under licence. On 23 August 2006 Imperial bought the **worldwide** Davidoff cigarette trademark outright from Tchibo Holding AG for **€540 million** (£368 million), according to its own stock-exchange announcement. Imperial Tobacco is now called Imperial Brands. The rights to all other Davidoff tobacco products, cigars included, stayed with Oettinger Davidoff.
- **Fragrances.** Davidoff perfumes are made by Coty under licence, a relationship Coty says began in the 1980s. Coty renewed and extended the licence on 3 May 2023.
- **Coffee.** Davidoff Café is sold by the German coffee company Tchibo. Sources disagree on whether Tchibo owns that part of the name or uses it under licence, so we only say who sells it.
- **Everything else.** A separate Swiss company, Zino Davidoff SA, founded in 1980, handles the lifestyle side of the name: fragrances, eyewear, leather goods and more.

## How it started

The Basel company is older than the Davidoff brand. **Max Oettinger** opened a cigar shop at Eisengasse 9 in Basel in 1875, which is why the company celebrated its 150th anniversary in 2025.

**Zino Davidoff** was born on 11 March 1906 in what is now Ukraine (sources disagree on the exact town). In 1911 his family moved to Geneva, where his father opened a small tobacco shop on the Place des Philosophes.

In the 1940s Zino Davidoff started a series of Cuban cigars named after Bordeaux wine estates. From 1969 cigars under his own name were made in Havana, at the El Laguito factory. In 1970 Oettinger took over Zino Davidoff's Geneva business, and Zino stayed on as the face of the brand.

## The break with Cuba

By the late 1980s Davidoff was buying about 11 million of Cuba's 70 million cigars a year, according to TIME magazine in April 1990. In 1989 it cancelled the arrangement. Zino Davidoff told TIME: "The fact is, the Cubans don't produce the same quality anymore."

What happened next became a legend: about **130,000** Cuban-made Davidoff cigars were reportedly burned in a bonfire. Cigar Aficionado reported the figure in 2005 but suggested that the real reason for the break may have been a dispute with Cuba over who owned the brand. That is why we say "reportedly".

Cuban production ended, and **since 1991** all Davidoff cigars have been made in the Dominican Republic, according to the company. Zino Davidoff died in Geneva on 14 January 1994.

## What we could not confirm

- Whether the 2014 non-family stake still exists.
- Who owns Zino Davidoff SA today: it describes itself as a Swiss family business but does not say which family.
- Whether Tchibo owns or licenses the Davidoff coffee name.
- Zino Davidoff's exact birthplace: some sources say Kyiv, others Novhorod-Siverskyi.

## Questions people ask

### Is Davidoff owned by Imperial Brands?

Only the cigarettes. Imperial Brands owns the worldwide Davidoff cigarette trademark, bought in 2006. The cigars belong to Oettinger Davidoff AG in Basel.

### Is Davidoff still a family company?

The cigar company is. Oettinger Davidoff AG is owned by the Schneider family, and in January 2026 the share capital passed to the next generation of that family.

### Are Davidoff cigars Cuban?

Not since 1989–1991. Davidoff broke with Cuba in 1989, and by 1991 all its cigars were made in the Dominican Republic.

### Who was Zino Davidoff?

A Geneva tobacconist, born in 1906 in what is now Ukraine, who gave his name to the brand. He sold his business to Oettinger in 1970, stayed on as its ambassador and died in 1994.

### Who makes Davidoff perfume?

Coty, under a licence it renewed in May 2023.
