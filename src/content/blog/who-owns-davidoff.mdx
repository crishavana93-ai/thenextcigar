*This is the full story behind our "Who owns it?" video on Davidoff. Every fact below has a source at the end. Business history, not advice. We don't sell tobacco.*

## The short answer

There is no single owner of "Davidoff". The name is split four ways:

| What | Who owns or runs it |
|---|---|
| **Cigars** | Oettinger Davidoff AG, Basel, a family company owned by the Schneider family |
| **Cigarettes** | Imperial Brands (formerly Imperial Tobacco), which bought the worldwide cigarette trademark in 2006 |
| **Fragrances** | Made by Coty under licence |
| **Coffee** | Sold by Tchibo |

## The cigar company

Oettinger Davidoff AG is registered in Basel, Switzerland. Its 2025 sales were **CHF 545.3 million**, up 2.5% in real terms, according to the company's own annual release of 1 July 2026.

In January 2026 the two daughters of Dr Ernst Schneider, the man who built the company, transferred the **entire share capital** to their direct descendants, the next generation of the Schneider family. Trade press reported that the management team did not change.

## Why the name is split

In 2006 Imperial Tobacco, now called Imperial Brands, paid **€540 million** for the worldwide rights to the Davidoff cigarette brand. That is why Davidoff cigarettes and Davidoff cigars have different owners.

The fragrances are made by Coty under a licence that Coty renewed and extended in May 2023. Davidoff coffee is sold by the German coffee company Tchibo.

## Timeline

- **1906:** Zino Davidoff is born, in what is now Ukraine. He grows up around his father's tobacco shop in Geneva.
- **1970:** Zino Davidoff sells the business to Oettinger, a Basel company run by Ernst Schneider.
- **1989:** Davidoff breaks with Cuba. Around 130,000 cigars were reportedly burned. (Cigar Aficionado reported the figure but doubted parts of the official story, which is why we say "reportedly".)
- **By 1991:** all Davidoff cigars are made in the Dominican Republic.
- **1994:** Zino Davidoff dies.
- **2006:** Imperial Tobacco buys the worldwide Davidoff cigarette trademark for €540 million.
- **May 2023:** Coty renews its Davidoff fragrance licence.
- **January 2026:** the Schneider daughters hand the entire share capital to the next generation.
- **July 2026:** Oettinger Davidoff reports CHF 545.3 million in sales for 2025.

## Questions people ask

### Is Davidoff owned by Imperial Brands?

Only the cigarettes. Imperial Brands owns the worldwide Davidoff cigarette trademark, bought in 2006. The cigars belong to Oettinger Davidoff AG in Basel.

### Is Davidoff still a family company?

The cigar company is. Oettinger Davidoff AG is owned by the Schneider family, and in January 2026 the share capital passed to the next generation of that family.

### Are Davidoff cigars Cuban?

Not since 1989–1991. Davidoff broke with Cuba in 1989, and by 1991 all its cigars were made in the Dominican Republic.
