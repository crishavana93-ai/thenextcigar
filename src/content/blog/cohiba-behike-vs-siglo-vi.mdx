**In short:** The Cohiba **Behike 52** and the Cohiba **Siglo VI** are both 52-ring-gauge cigars, but they belong to different Cohiba lines. The Behike 52 is the shortest of the three Behike sizes launched in **2010**; Cigar Aficionado reported that its blend uses a large share of *medio tiempo* leaf. The Siglo VI was added to Cohiba's **Línea 1492** in **2002** and is longer, in a size Cuban factories call a *cañonazo*.

## The Behike 52

The Behike line has three sizes, the BHK 52, 54 and 56; the number is the ring gauge. Cigar Aficionado reported at launch that the blend uses a large share of *medio tiempo*, the uppermost leaves of the tobacco plant, from farms near San Juan y Martínez and San Luis in Pinar del Río. Halfwheel notes the line debuted in 2010 for Cohiba's 45th anniversary. The three sizes are compared in [Cohiba Behike 52, 54 and 56](/blog/cohiba-behike-52-vs-54-vs-56-which-to-buy-2026/).

## The Siglo VI

The Siglo VI belongs to the line officially named Línea 1492, often called the "Siglo series". According to Cigar Aficionado, Habanos attributes the line's character to filler leaf that spends extra time fermenting in barrels and to tobacco grown on five farms in the Vuelta Abajo, Pinar del Río.

## Questions

### What is the difference between the Behike 52 and the Siglo VI?

Both have a 52 ring gauge. The Behike 52 is shorter (119 mm) and belongs to the Behike line launched in 2010; the Siglo VI is longer (149 mm) and belongs to the Línea 1492, to which it was added in 2002.

### What is medio tiempo tobacco?

The uppermost leaves of the tobacco plant. Cigar Aficionado reported in 2010 that the Behike blend uses a large share of it.

### What is a cañonazo?

The name Cuban factories use for the Siglo VI's size, a 52-ring cigar of 5 7/8 inches. It means "cannon shot".
