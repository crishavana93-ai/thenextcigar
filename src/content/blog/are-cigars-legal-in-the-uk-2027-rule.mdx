**In short:** Yes. Cigars, Cuban cigars included, are legal in the UK, and today they can be sold to anyone aged 18 or over. That is about to change. From **1 January 2027**, the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 makes it an offence anywhere in the UK to sell a tobacco product, cigars included, to anyone **born on or after 1 January 2009**. The line never moves, so the group who can never legally be sold tobacco grows every year.

*Checked against the legislation on 6 November 2026. This is general information, not legal advice.*

## The buying age today

In England and Wales the minimum age to be sold tobacco has been 18 since 2007, when an order replaced "sixteen" with "eighteen" in the Children and Young Persons Act 1933 ([SI 2007/767](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/767/made)). Scotland's law also sets the line at 18 (Tobacco and Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act 2010, section 4), and Northern Ireland has its own legislation.

## What changes on 1 January 2027

The Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 replaces the age limit with a birth date. Section 1 reads: "It is an offence to sell any of the following to a person born on or after 1 January 2009— (a) a tobacco product" ([s.1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/1/enacted)).

- **England and Wales:** section 1.
- **Scotland:** section 52 amends the 2010 Act, replacing "under the age of 18" with "born on or after 1 January 2009" ([s.52](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/52/enacted)).
- **Northern Ireland:** section 72 sets the same rule ([s.72](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/72/enacted)).
- **Start date:** all three come into force on 1 January 2027 ([s.175](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/175/enacted)).

Because the date is fixed, the youngest person who can legally be sold tobacco gets older every year:

| On 1 January | Youngest age that can be sold tobacco |
|---|---|
| 2027 | 18 |
| 2030 | 21 |
| 2035 | 26 |
| 2040 | 31 |
| 2050 | 41 |

*Our arithmetic from section 1, for someone born on 31 December 2008.*

The Act does not make smoking or owning a cigar an offence. It makes it an offence to sell one to someone born on or after 1 January 2009, or for an adult to buy one on their behalf ([s.2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/18/section/2/enacted); s.52(4) in Scotland; s.73 in Northern Ireland). Buying tobacco on behalf of under-18s is already an offence today.

## Smoke-free rules

In England, the Health Act 2006 makes premises smoke-free if they are open to the public or used as a workplace, but "only in those areas which are enclosed or substantially enclosed" ([Health Act 2006, s.2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/28/section/2)). Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have their own smoke-free laws. The 2026 Act gives each government power to extend smoke-free places by regulations (Part 7); none of those powers had been used by 6 November 2026.

## Arriving in Great Britain

Travellers arriving in Great Britain from outside the UK, including from the EU, have a personal tobacco allowance of one of: 200 cigarettes, 100 cigarillos, 50 cigars, 250 g of tobacco or 200 sticks for heated-tobacco devices, which can be split between categories ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/duty-free-goods/arriving-in-Great-Britain)). Over the allowance, tax and duty are due on all the goods in that category, not just the excess. There is no tobacco allowance for under-17s. Northern Ireland has separate rules.

The US embargo on Cuba is American law and does not apply in the UK, so Cuban cigars are legal there like any other cigar. ## Questions

### Are cigars legal in the UK?

Yes. Adults can buy and smoke cigars. From 1 January 2027 it is an offence to sell tobacco, cigars included, to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009.

### Will cigars be banned in the UK?

No outright ban. From 1 January 2027 the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 makes it an offence to sell tobacco to anyone born on or after 1 January 2009, or for an adult to buy it on their behalf. People born before that date can still be sold tobacco.

### Can I smoke a cigar in a pub in England?

Not inside. Premises open to the public are smoke-free in areas that are enclosed or substantially enclosed. Outdoor areas that are not substantially enclosed are not covered by that rule.

### What is the tobacco allowance for Great Britain?

GOV.UK sets a personal allowance of one category, for example 50 cigars, or a split between categories. Northern Ireland has separate rules.
