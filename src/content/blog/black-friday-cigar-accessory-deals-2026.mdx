## What's worth buying on sale, and what isn't

**Worth waiting for:** the bigger-ticket items. A humidor cabinet or a tabletop lighter is where a percentage discount is worth real money. Two of them already cost less than when we checked in October: the Woodronic cabinet was $149.99 on 19 October and $132.99 on 8 November, and the Case Elegance Luca went from $178.99 to $166.99.

**Not worth waiting for:** humidity packs and a hygrometer. At $8–$12 the saving is small, and a humidor without them is the bigger risk. If your packs are hard, buy them now.
