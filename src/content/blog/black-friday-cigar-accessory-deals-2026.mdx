## How to tell a real deal from a fake one

A crossed-out price on its own tells you little. Three checks take two minutes:

1. **Look before the sale.** Open the product page now, in the week before Black Friday, and note the price. On the day, compare against that number, not against the crossed-out one.
2. **Read what the crossed-out price is.** Amazon labels it, for example as "List" or "Typical price". Hover or tap the label to see how Amazon defines it. A discount against a list price nobody pays is not a saving.
3. **Check the seller.** "Ships from and sold by Amazon.com" or the brand's own store is safer than an unknown third-party seller offering the lowest number.

## What's worth waiting for, and what isn't

**Worth waiting for:** the bigger-ticket items. A humidor cabinet or a tabletop lighter is where a percentage discount is worth real money, and their prices move both ways in the weeks before the sale, so the check in step 1 matters most here.

**Not worth waiting for:** humidity packs and a hygrometer. They are cheap, so the saving is small, and a humidor without them is the bigger risk. If your packs are hard, buy them now.
