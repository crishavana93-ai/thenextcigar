## What's worth buying on sale, and what isn't

**Worth waiting for:** the bigger-ticket items. A humidor cabinet or a tabletop lighter is where a percentage discount is worth real money. Prices on these move both ways: the Woodronic cabinet was $149.99 on 19 October, $132.99 on 8 November, $169.99 on 10 November and $132.99 again on 12 November. The Case Elegance Luca went from $178.99 to $166.99, then $165.35, then $166.59.

**Not worth waiting for:** humidity packs and a hygrometer. At $8–$12 the saving is small, and a humidor without them is the bigger risk. If your packs are hard, buy them now.
