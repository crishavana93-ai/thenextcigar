**In short:** For more than two decades, Habanos S.A. used one week in Havana, the Festival del Habano, to show the world its new cigars. In 2026 there was no festival. The 26th edition, planned for **23–27 February**, was postponed on 14 February and cancelled on **20 July**, as Cuba's energy crisis dragged on. So Habanos has taken its headline launches on tour instead: **Cannes, London, Madrid and Macau** between late September and mid-November, with **Abidjan** next on **21 November**. One of them is a first for its brand: a permanent cigar sold only through Habanos' own shop network.

## The launch map

| When | Where | What Habanos launched |
|---|---|---|
| ~30 Sep 2026 | Cannes, France, at the TFWA duty-free trade show | **Montecristo Fragata**, a new size sold only in duty-free shops |
| 7 Oct 2026 | London, about 400 guests | **Cohiba Talismán** becomes a regular cigar, opening Cohiba's 60th year |
| 24 Oct 2026 | Madrid, Las Ventas bullring, 400+ guests | **Quai d'Orsay Concordia**, the first Edición Limitada of 2026 |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Macau, "more than 500" guests | **Saint Luis Rey Palatinos**, sold only through La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist shops |
| 21 Nov 2026 | Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire | **Bolívar Safari**, the first Regional Edition for all of Africa |

*Sources: Cigar Aficionado (1 Oct, 13 Oct, 27 Oct and 16 Nov 2026); Habanos S.A. press releases of 8 October, 27 October and 16 November 2026 (PR Newswire).*

We covered two of these launches in detail: [the Talismán in London](/blog/cohiba-talisman-regular-production-2026/) and [the Concordia in Madrid](/blog/quai-dorsay-concordia-edicion-limitada-2026/).

## What is actually new

**A cigar only the company's own shops can sell.** Habanos describes the Saint Luis Rey Palatinos as "the first regular-production vitola in the brand's history to be available exclusively through La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist establishments," according to *Cigar Aficionado*. It measures 140 mm by 52 ring gauge, according to Habanos' release. *Cigar Aficionado* puts the network at more than 150 La Casa del Habano shops and more than 1,200 Habanos Specialists worldwide. Habanos already releases a cigar exclusive to these shops each year, such as the Trinidad Villa launched in Mexico City in November 2025; what is new is that Habanos calls Palatinos a regular-production cigar.

**Channel-specific cigars.** Two of the five launches are tied to where they are sold, not just to a brand: the Fragata to duty-free shops, the Palatinos to the franchise network. A third, the Bolívar Safari, is tied to a region. *Cigar Aficionado* notes that earlier Regional Editions were made for countries and territories, including South Africa, but there "has never been one for the entire continent of Africa."

**The charity auction moved to Macau.** The Festival del Habano's humidor auction usually raises money for Cuba's public health system. At the Macau gala, four humidors sold for **US$753,750**, with the proceeds going to Cuba's public health system, according to Habanos' release.

## What we still don't know for 2027

- **Whether there will be a festival.** Habanos said the next edition will be held "when conditions allow the Festival to be presented," according to *Cigar Aficionado*. No date has been announced.
- **Where next year's new cigars will be shown.** Habanos described the London launch as the "first major milestone" of Cohiba's 60th year, "with an exciting year of new releases ahead," but named no further cities or dates.
- **Prices.** *Cigar Aficionado* reports that prices for the Palatinos have not been set. We don't forecast prices.
