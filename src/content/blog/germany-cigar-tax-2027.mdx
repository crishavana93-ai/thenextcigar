**In short:** Germany has raised its tax on cigars and cigarillos. The Bundestag passed the law on **15 October 2026** and it was published in the Federal Law Gazette on **20 October** (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 309). For cigars and cigarillos, the new rates start on **1 March 2027** and rise again each January until **2030**. The fixed part of the tax doubles, from **1.4 to 2.85 cents** a piece, the share of the retail price rises from **1.47% to 3.83%**, and the minimum per piece goes from **7.504 to 13.964 cents**. A late proposal that, according to the cigar makers' association, would have raised the price-based share for cigars to 21.05% was dropped, though the final law sets higher minimums than the government's draft.

## How Germany taxes a cigar

German tax on a cigar or cigarillo has two parts: a fixed amount per piece, plus a percentage of the retail price. If the two together come to less than a set minimum, the minimum applies. The minimum is stated "less the VAT in the retail price" of the cigar.

## The new rates

| Period | Per piece | Share of retail price | Minimum per piece |
|---|---|---|---|
| Until 28 Feb 2027 | 1.4 cents | 1.47% | 7.504 cents |
| 1 Mar – 31 Dec 2027 | 2.14 cents | 3.83% | 10.059 cents |
| 2028 | 2.35 cents | 3.83% | 11.258 cents |
| 2029 | 2.59 cents | 3.83% | 12.488 cents |
| From 2030 | 2.85 cents | 3.83% | 13.964 cents |

*Minimums are each less the VAT in the retail price. The law entered into force on 21 October 2026 through what the finance ministry called a technical error in its start date; Germany's customs administration says the old rates apply until 31 December 2026 and, for cigars and cigarillos, until 28 February 2027 ([beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtspolitik-gesetzgebung/tabaksteuergesetz-bundesfinanzministerium-inkrafttreten-patzer-2026-10-21)). Source: Tabaksteuergesetz § 2(1) no. 2, as amended by the law of 19 October 2026, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 309 ([consolidated text](https://www.buzer.de/2_TabStG.htm)). The current rates are also set out in the cigar makers' association's [statement to the Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/1221732/03-BdZ.pdf).*

By 2030 the fixed part is a little more than double today's (2.85 ÷ 1.4), the price-based share is 2.6 times higher (3.83 ÷ 1.47), and the minimum is 1.86 times higher (13.964 ÷ 7.504).

## What cigars avoided

The government's draft, [Drucksache 21/7859](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/078/2107859.pdf) of 7 September 2026, already raised the cigar rates. Before the vote, the coalition parties CDU/CSU and SPD prepared an amendment. According to the [Bundesverband der Zigarrenindustrie](https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/1221732/03-BdZ.pdf) (BdZ), the German cigar makers' association, the draft wording it had seen would have raised the price-based part of the cigar tax from 1.47% to 21.05%, an increase of more than 1,300%, and the tax on some cigars up to twentyfold. The association warned of retail price rises of 50 to 60%.

The final law kept the government draft's price-based share for cigars at 3.83% and its per-piece steps, but the finance committee raised the minimums above the draft (13.964 cents from 2030, against 9.69 cents in the draft) and moved the start for cigars, cigarillos, pipe tobacco and waterpipe tobacco to 1 March 2027, according to the [Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw42-de-tabaksteuer-1224154) and the committee's report, Drucksache 21/8605. For cigarettes, the coalition's higher rates stayed in the final law. The US site [halfwheel](https://halfwheel.com/report-germany-gives-cigars-pipes-a-reprieve-from-massive-tobacco-tax-increase/481310/) called it a reprieve for cigars and pipes.

## The German cigar market, in the industry's numbers

In its statement to the Bundestag on 1 October 2026, the BdZ said:

- **2.1 billion** cigars and cigarillos were taxed in Germany in 2025, down from **6.5 billion** in 2007.
- The industry has about **1,600 employees** under collective agreements.
- Its 22 member companies are mostly medium-sized family businesses, with production mainly in North Rhine-Westphalia, Lower Saxony, Hesse, Baden-Württemberg and Thuringia.

These are the association's own figures.

## How it fits with the EU

The EU is negotiating its own minimum taxes on tobacco, including cigars. The Council presidency's compromise text of 6 November 2026 ([ST 15388/26](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-15388-2026-INIT/en/pdf), Article 20) would raise the EU minimum for cigars and cigarillos from €12 to €80 per 1,000, or 30% of the retail price, from 2032. EU finance ministers failed to agree the package on 9 November, when Malta blocked it. The BdZ asked the Bundestag to base Germany's rates on the German market rather than on those EU talks. More: [EU Tobacco Tax Reform and Cigars](/blog/eu-tobacco-tax-proposal-a-1-000-hike-on-the-horizon/) and [Malta blocks the EU deal](/blog/eu-cigar-tax-malta-blocks-deal-november-2026/).

## What happens next

- **1 March 2027:** the first rise for cigars and cigarillos.
- **1 January 2028, 2029 and 2030:** the next steps.

What the tax change will do to retail prices is up to manufacturers and retailers. We don't forecast prices.
