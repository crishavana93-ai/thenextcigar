## What not to buy

- **Accessories carrying a cigar brand's logo.** They cost more for the name, and the smoker may not smoke that brand.
- **A humidor larger than they need.** A half-empty cabinet in a small apartment is a burden. If they buy a box a year, the Woodronic or Luca is enough.
- **Cigars as a surprise.** Unless you know exactly what they smoke, accessories make the safer gift.
