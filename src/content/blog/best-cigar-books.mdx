## The handbooks

### 1. The Ultimate Cigar Book, 4th Edition, by Richard Carleton Hacker

The standard one-volume handbook, now in its fourth edition: how cigars are grown and made, how to choose, cut, light and store them, and what to drink with them. It has more than 2,300 ratings on Amazon, several times more than any other book on this list, and at $13 it is also the cheapest hardcover here.

Skyhorse, 2015, 392 pages, hardcover. **$13.00. 4.8 stars from 2,381 ratings.**
**Audiobook:** narrated by Peter Coates, 6 hours 56 minutes.

[See The Ultimate Cigar Book on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1632206579?tag=thenextcigar-20) · [Audiobook →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DJC4D4XW?tag=thenextcigar-20)

### 2. The Cigar Companion, Third Edition, by Anwer Bati and Simon Chase

An illustrated guide, subtitled "the connoisseur's guide" and now in its third edition. A good one to dip into rather than read straight through.

Chartwell Books, 2020, 224 pages, hardcover. **$21.96. 4.7 stars from 347 ratings.**

[See The Cigar Companion on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0785838422?tag=thenextcigar-20)

### 3. The Cuban Cigar Handbook, by Matteo Speranza

The only book on this list about Cuban cigars alone, subtitled "the discerning aficionado's guide to the best Cuban cigars in the world". The natural choice for someone who smokes Habanos and already knows the basics.

Cider Mill Press, 2021, 304 pages, paperback. **$14.01 (hardcover $19.99). 4.8 stars from 195 ratings.**
**Audiobook:** narrated by Christopher Salazar, 4 hours 54 minutes.

[See The Cuban Cigar Handbook on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1646431065?tag=thenextcigar-20) · [Audiobook →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F1VTL5F6?tag=thenextcigar-20)

### 4. Tobacconist Handbook, by Jorge Armenteros

Subtitled "an essential guide to cigars and pipes". The one to choose for someone who smokes a pipe as well, since the other handbooks here are about cigars only.

Skyhorse, 2020, 264 pages, hardcover. **$31.38. 4.6 stars from 306 ratings.**

[See Tobacconist Handbook on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1510752129?tag=thenextcigar-20)

## The one for the coffee table

### 5. Cigars: A Guide, by Nicholas Foulkes

An illustrated hardcover from a British writer on luxury and style. It costs the most of the seven, and it is the one to choose when the book will sit on a table as much as be read.

Preface Publishing, 2018, 256 pages, hardcover. **$36.19. 4.7 stars from 162 ratings.**

[See Cigars: A Guide on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1848094876?tag=thenextcigar-20)

## Two histories

### 6. The Cigar: An Illustrated History of Fine Smoking, by Barnaby Conrad

A short illustrated history from 1996. At 132 pages it is quick to read and heavy on pictures. It is an older title, so check who is selling it and in what condition.

Chronicle Books, 1996, 132 pages, hardcover. **$27.42. 4.5 stars from 84 ratings.**

[See The Cigar on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0811814491?tag=thenextcigar-20)

### 7. Churchill's Cigar, by Stephen McGinty

Subtitled "a lifelong love affair through war and peace": Winston Churchill's life followed through the cigars he smoked. For the reader who prefers biography to a handbook.

Macmillan, 2013, 250 pages, paperback. **$16.94. 4.2 stars from 53 ratings.**

[See Churchill's Cigar on Amazon →](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0230772447?tag=thenextcigar-20)

## Quick answers

**What is the best cigar book for a beginner?** *The Ultimate Cigar Book* by Richard Carleton Hacker. It covers the whole subject in one volume and is the most-rated cigar book on Amazon (4.8 stars from 2,381 ratings on 10 November 2026).

**Is there a book just about Cuban cigars?** Yes: *The Cuban Cigar Handbook* by Matteo Speranza (Cider Mill Press, 2021).

**Are any cigar books available as audiobooks?** Two on this list: *The Ultimate Cigar Book* (6 hours 56 minutes) and *The Cuban Cigar Handbook* (4 hours 54 minutes).

**What is a good cigar book as a gift?** For most people, *The Ultimate Cigar Book* at $13. For a book to leave on a table, *Cigars: A Guide* by Nicholas Foulkes.
