**In short:** Sweden's 2019 tobacco law leaves few places to smoke a cigar in public, so cigar smokers meet in lounges: a members' club in Stockholm and a Cuban-themed hotel with a cigar lounge in Varberg. Cuban cigars are scarce: the official Nordic importer lost its tobacco licence in September 2026 and is being wound up. Details below were checked on each venue's own website on 2 November 2026.

## The legal background

Sweden's 2019 tobacco law extended smoking bans to outdoor serving areas, entrances, platforms and bus stops, so there are few places to smoke a cigar in public. Cigar lounges and smoking rooms operate under the same law. If you are planning a visit for a particular lounge, check its own booking rules first. The [Public Health Agency of Sweden](https://www.folkhalsomyndigheten.se/the-public-health-agency-of-sweden/) publishes the current rules.

## Stockholm: Cigarrklubben

**Cigarrklubben.** Fleminggatan 15, Stockholm. A private members' club, open around the clock to members. Non-members can visit on Fridays and Saturdays, 12–18, by registering and booking in advance. Cigarrklubben also publishes the trade magazine Cigarrvärlden.

## Varberg: Hotell Havanna

**Hotell Havanna.** Krabbes väg 4, 432 52 Varberg. A Cuban-themed hotel on the west coast, about an hour south of Gothenburg by car, with a cigar lounge (Cigarrsalongen), Hemingway's bar and rum tastings. Lounge hours and bookings are on the hotel's website.

## The Cuban supply situation

Elite Trading Scandinavia (formerly Habanos Nordic), the Gothenburg company that imported Cuban cigars for the Nordics and Baltics, is in voluntary liquidation. According to Realtid (22 October 2026):

- Gothenburg municipality revoked its wholesale tobacco licence on 15 December 2025. The company appealed, lost in the administrative court on 10 June 2026, and lost the licence for good on 11 September 2026.
- Shareholders voted for voluntary liquidation on 1 July 2026.
- The trade site Cigarrvärlden estimates the company held about 45% of the Swedish cigar market.
- No successor importer has been named.

The background, including the link to a sanctioned part-owner of Habanos S.A., is in our report: [Sweden's Cuban cigar importer is being wound up](/blog/sweden-cuban-cigar-importer-liquidation-2026/).
