**In short:** Sweden has three Brobergs shops (Stockholm, Gothenburg and, since 22 October 2026, a new address in Malmö), a members' cigar lounge in Stockholm, and a Cuban-themed hotel with a cigar lounge in Varberg. Cuban cigars are scarce: the official Nordic importer lost its tobacco licence in September 2026 and is being wound up. Addresses and hours below were checked on each business's own website on 2 November 2026.

## The Cuban supply situation

Elite Trading Scandinavia (formerly Habanos Nordic), the Gothenburg company that imported Cuban cigars for the Nordics and Baltics, is in voluntary liquidation. According to Realtid (22 October 2026):

- Gothenburg municipality revoked its wholesale tobacco licence on 15 December 2025. The company appealed, lost in the administrative court on 10 June 2026, and lost the licence for good on 11 September 2026.
- Shareholders voted for voluntary liquidation on 1 July 2026.
- The trade site Cigarrvärlden estimates the company held about 45% of the Swedish cigar market.
- Other routes remain, such as smaller importers and private imports, but the official channel is closed. No successor importer has been named.

The background, including the link to a sanctioned part-owner of Habanos S.A., is in our report: [Sweden's Cuban cigar importer is being wound up](/blog/sweden-cuban-cigar-importer-liquidation-2026/). In practice, call a shop before travelling for a specific Cuban cigar.

## The legal background

Sweden's 2019 tobacco law extended smoking bans to outdoor serving areas, entrances, platforms and bus stops, so there are few places to smoke a cigar in public. Cigar lounges and smoking rooms operate under the same law. If you are planning a visit for a particular lounge, check its own booking rules first. The [Public Health Agency of Sweden](https://www.folkhalsomyndigheten.se/the-public-health-agency-of-sweden/) publishes the current rules.

## Stockholm

**Brobergs Tobakshandel, Sturegallerian.** Sturegallerian 39, 114 46 Stockholm. Open Monday to Friday 10–19, Saturday 10–17, Sunday 12–17. Brobergs says its first shop opened in Gothenburg in 1881. The Stockholm shop sells cigars, humidors and accessories.

**Cigarrklubben.** Fleminggatan 15, Stockholm. A private members' club, open around the clock to members. Non-members can visit on Fridays and Saturdays, 12–18, by registering and booking in advance. Cigarrklubben also runs a webshop and publishes the trade magazine Cigarrvärlden.

## Gothenburg

**Brobergs Tobakshandel, Arkaden.** Arkaden, 411 07 Gothenburg. Open Monday to Friday 10–19, Saturday 10–18, Sunday 11–17. This is the city where Brobergs started.

**Sweets'N Cigars.** Redbergsvägen 8, 416 65 Gothenburg. We could not confirm in November 2026 that it is still trading; its website did not load for us. Call before you go.

## Malmö

**Brobergs Tobakshandel, Baltzarsgatan.** Baltzarsgatan 30, 211 36 Malmö. Brobergs moved its Malmö shop here from Triangeln on 22 October 2026. Open Monday to Friday 10–19, Saturday 10–17, Sunday 11–16. It is the nearest of the three Brobergs shops for visitors coming over the Øresund Bridge from Copenhagen.

## Varberg

**Hotell Havanna.** Krabbes väg 4, 432 52 Varberg. A Cuban-themed hotel on the west coast, about an hour south of Gothenburg by car, with a cigar lounge (Cigarrsalongen), Hemingway's bar and rum tastings. Lounge hours and bookings are on the hotel's website.

## Buying online in Sweden

**Cigarrspecialisten.se** says it buys from official suppliers and has an agreement with Habanos S.A.

**Puros.se** is another Swedish online cigar retailer.

Swedish law requires buyers of tobacco to be 18 or over. With the official importer gone, check stated stock before ordering Cuban cigars.

## Before you go

- Opening hours change around holidays; each Brobergs shop lists exceptions on [brobergs.se](https://www.brobergs.se/page/vara-butiker).
- Cuban stock is the thing most likely to have changed. Call ahead.
