**In short:** Habanos S.A. premiered the **Quai d'Orsay Concordia Edición Limitada 2026** on **24 October 2026** at the Las Ventas bullring in Madrid. It is 145 mm long with a 50 ring gauge, comes in boxes of 20, and has a recommended retail price of **€914 a box**. It is Quai d'Orsay's second Edición Limitada, after the Senadores in 2019, and so far it is the only Edición Limitada Habanos has announced for 2026.

## What Habanos announced

In a press release datelined Havana, 28 October 2026, Habanos said the Concordia had its world premiere at an evening called "La Nuit D'Orsay", hosted by Club Pasión Habanos for more than 400 guests.

| | Quai d'Orsay Concordia Edición Limitada 2026 |
|---|---|
| Factory size (vitola de galera) | Preciosos |
| Length | 145 mm (5¾ in) |
| Ring gauge | 50 |
| Shape | Parejo |
| Box | 20 cigars |
| Recommended retail price | €914 a box (about €45.70 a cigar) |

*Source: Habanos S.A. press release, 28 October 2026. Cigar Aficionado reports the same size and price.*

Habanos says the tobacco comes from Vuelta Abajo in Pinar del Río and that the cigar is made entirely by hand with long filler. As with every Edición Limitada, Habanos says the wrapper, binder and filler leaves were aged for at least two more years, and that the wrapper had a longer fermentation, which makes it darker.

## Why it matters

**It may be the only Edición Limitada this year.** Habanos usually releases about three Limited Editions a year but has skipped some years, including 2002, 2022 and 2023. When Cigar Aficionado first reported the Concordia in July, it called it the first Edición Limitada of 2026 "and possibly the only one". The Habanos Festival in Havana, where releases are normally announced, did not take place this year.

**It is a rare size.** Cigar Aficionado says Habanos has used the Preciosos size only once before, for the Montecristo L'Esprit in 2023.

**Quai d'Orsay has done this once before.** The brand dates from 1974, when it was made for the French and Swiss markets. Its only earlier Edición Limitada was the Senadores in 2019.

## Where and when it's sold

- **Spain first.** The launch was organised by Tabacalera, Habanos' distributor for Spain. Cigars Connect notes that the country hosting a premiere usually gets a period of exclusivity, lasting weeks or months.
- **Elsewhere:** Habanos gave no dates for other markets. It sells through importers in more than 130 countries and territories, and each importer decides when stock arrives and at what price.
- **Sweden and the Nordics:** the Nordic importer, Elite Trading Scandinavia (formerly Habanos Nordic), is in voluntary liquidation and no successor has been named ([our report](/blog/sweden-cuban-cigar-importer-liquidation-2026/)), so there is no date for the Concordia in Swedish shops.
