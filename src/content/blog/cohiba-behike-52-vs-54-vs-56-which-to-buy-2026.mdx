**In short:** The Cohiba Behike line has three sizes, all launched in 2010 for the brand's 45th anniversary: the **BHK 52**, **BHK 54** and **BHK 56**. The number is the ring gauge. They differ in length and thickness; Cigar Aficionado's launch report describes the same blend family for all three, with a large share of *medio tiempo* leaf.

## The three sizes side by side

| | BHK 52 | BHK 54 | BHK 56 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 4 11/16 in (119 mm) | 5 2/3 in (144 mm) | 6 1/2 in (165 mm) |
| Ring gauge | 52 | 54 | 56 |
| Factory name (vitola de galera) | Laguito No. 4 | Laguito No. 5 | Laguito No. 6 |
| Box | 10, lacquered | 10, lacquered | 10, lacquered |

*Source: Cigar Aficionado, 24 February 2010. Millimetres converted from inches.*

## What the three share

- **Medio tiempo leaf.** Cigar Aficionado reported at launch that the blend uses a large share of *medio tiempo*, the upper leaves of the tobacco plant, from farms near San Juan y Martínez and San Luis in Pinar del Río.
- **The anniversary.** Halfwheel notes the line debuted in 2010 to mark Cohiba's 45th anniversary. A different cigar with a similar name, the Cohiba Behike of 2006, was a limited release for the 40th anniversary; it is not the same cigar.
- **The recognition.** Cigar Aficionado named the BHK 52 its Cigar of the Year in 2010.
