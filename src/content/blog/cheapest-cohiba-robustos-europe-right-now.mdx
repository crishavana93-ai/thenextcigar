## Why is Sweden's Cigarrummet the cheapest right now?

Three reasons.

**Stockholm allocation depth.** Habanos Nordic AB allocates to Stockholm retailers across multiple shops (Cigarrummet, Cigarrhyllan, Cigarrspecialisten). When one runs out, demand shifts to the next. Cigarrummet has been consistently in stock through 2026 because they pace their per-shop allocation tighter.

**SEK weakness against the euro.** The SEK 19,200 sticker hasn't moved much in nominal terms, but EUR/SEK has weakened — the same Swedish price now converts to €1,670 vs €1,790 at 2024 rates. That's a 7% FX-driven discount for euro-paying buyers if you can find a way to pay in SEK or accept the FX conversion at checkout.

**Lower retail mark-up structure.** Swedish Habanos retailers operate on thinner per-box mark-ups than LCDH-franchise stores in Switzerland and Germany. The trade-off is less aged inventory and less limited-edition stock — Sweden is for current-production Cohibas, not 5-year-old Behike Año Nuevo allocations.
