## What a V-cut does, and when not to use one

A straight cut takes the whole end of the cap off. A V-cut leaves most of the cap in place and cuts a wedge-shaped slot across it. Two things follow:

- **More cap stays on**, so the wrapper is held together at the head and is less likely to unravel.
- **The opening is a slot, not a circle**, so the draw is more concentrated than with a straight cut.

That suits cigars with a **rounded head and a thicker ring**: robustos, toros and gordos.

It doesn't suit everything:

- **Pointed heads** (torpedos, belicosos, pirámides). The V has little to cut across, so it's rarely the best choice. A straight cut, taking off only the tip, is simpler.
- **Thin cigars** (lanceros, panetelas). A full-depth wedge takes a large share of a small head. Use a straight cut, or hold the head further out of the V-cutter for a shallower wedge, as Colibri suggests for its SV-Cut.

## How to use a V-cutter

1. **Check the ring.** If the cigar doesn't sit comfortably in the opening, don't force it. Use a straight cutter instead.
2. **Centre the head.** Rest the rounded head in the bowl so the blade will cross the middle of the cap.
3. **Press once, firmly.** A spring-loaded cutter like the Colibri needs one clean press. Hesitating tears the wrapper.
4. **Clear the blade.** Tap out the bit of tobacco left in the V. A blade full of debris cuts worse next time.

## V-cutter, straight cut or punch?

If you only own one cutter, make it a straight cut: it works on every shape. A V-cutter is the **second** cutter, for the thicker, rounded cigars where it does its best work. That is why the SV-Cut, which carries both blades, is the most versatile tool here.

## Quick answers

**What is the best V-cut cigar cutter?** For most smokers, the Colibri Premium V-Cut: 60-plus ring gauge, up to 7 mm deep, a two-year warranty and 4.8 stars from more than 5,000 Amazon ratings. For the biggest rings (64 and up), the Xikar VX2.

**What ring gauge can a V-cutter take?** It depends on the model. Most here are rated around 60. The Xikar VX2 is rated for 64, and its listing says it clips up to 70 at the head.

**Is a V-cut better than a straight cut?** Not better, different. A V-cut concentrates the draw and keeps more of the cap on rounded heads; a straight cut works on every shape and is the only sensible cut on a pointed head.

**Can you V-cut a torpedo?** Not well. The pointed head gives the blade nothing to cut across. Use a straight cut, taking off only the tip.

**Does a V-cutter need sharpening?** Not usually. Keep the blade clean. Xikar's warranty covers defects, not blade wear from normal use, and opening the cutter yourself voids it.
