## What not to buy

- **Cigars, unless you know exactly what they smoke.** Taste is personal, and in many countries tobacco can't be shipped as a gift.
- **"Cigar gift sets" with a cutter, lighter, ashtray and case in one box.** They look generous and usually contain four weak pieces. One good cutter beats four poor ones.
- **A humidor for someone who already has one.** Buy the Boveda pack and hygrometer instead.
