## What NOT to buy as a cigar gift

- **Cheap cutters with novelty handles.** They crush wrappers within ten cuts.
- **Cigar gear cross-branded with something else** (sports teams, motorbike brands). Smokers don't want their hobby branded by another one.
- **Cigars themselves**, unless you know their brand and strength exactly. Cigars are personal; the wrong one is worse than none.

## Questions people ask

**What do you get a cigar smoker who has everything?** Something they use up or lose: a second cutter for the travel case, a lighter for the car, a good journal. Avoid display pieces.

**Is it OK to give cigars as a gift?** Only if you know exactly what they smoke. Otherwise give the tools.

**Can I send a torch lighter as a gift?** Yes, but it ships empty: butane can't go in the post. Add a can of butane bought locally.
