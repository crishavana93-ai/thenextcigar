The guillotine is the cut most cigars are built for. Two blades close on the cap from opposite sides and meet in the middle, which is why a double blade tears less than a single one — nothing is pushing the wrapper sideways with nothing to push against.

This is the plain version of that tool. Stainless, double blade, no brand on the side, the shape that has been on every tobacconist counter for sixty years.
