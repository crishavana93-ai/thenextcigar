The complaint about pocket guillotines is always the same: the aperture is fine for a Petit Corona and a squeeze for a 54-ring Robusto Extra, and on a 60 it crushes before it cuts. A guillotine built with a larger opening fixes that one problem, and this is the one problem it is for.

This one carries a maker's name, Lubinski, which is more than most of what sits on our shelf. It comes in a gift box, and the pictured finish is a printed Cuban flag on the body — a matter of taste, and we will say so plainly: it is the only decorated object in the shop.

## What we know, and how we know it

The supplier's listing says a large-diameter double-blade guillotine, boxed. It has been sold twice through that listing and reviewed once, which is nothing to go on, so we do not repeat the listing's claims about steel or edge life. Ours is on order; when it lands this page gets our photographs, a measurement of the aperture with a ring-gauge tool, and a verdict on the cut.

## What it is not

It is not a V-cutter or a punch, and it does not fold. If you want one tool for a travel case, the [4-in-1 multitool](/shop/four-in-one-v-cutter-multitool/) is that object; this is a bench cutter you keep by the humidor.
