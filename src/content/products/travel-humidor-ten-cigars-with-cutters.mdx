A leather case is a weekend. A week is a different problem: by day four the cigars have given up whatever moisture they left home with, and a soft case does nothing about it. The hard travel humidor is the answer, and it is the only one of the three kinds in [our guide](/blog/cigar-travel-cases-which-to-buy-2026/) that is actually a humidor.

This one is the full kit. A latched hard shell, a foam tray cut for ten cigars, a hygrometer in the lid, two cutters, a punch, and a pair of two-way humidity packs to put in before you close it.

## What it is not

Not pocketable. It is a box, and it goes in luggage. If you carry two cigars to dinner, this is the wrong object; the [leather case](/shop/leather-four-cigar-case-with-tool-pockets/) is the right one.
