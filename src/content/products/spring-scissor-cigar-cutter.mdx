Scissors are the cut that came before the guillotine, and they are still the only tool that lets you stop halfway and think about it. A guillotine is commit-and-go. Scissors let you take a sliver, look at the cap, and take another.

They suit two situations in particular: a figurado, where the right depth is a judgement rather than a measurement, and a cigar you care about, where the ritual is part of the point.

## Why this one is here at all

Scissors are the gap in the market. Every shop stocks guillotines and punches; almost nobody writes about scissors seriously, which is odd given how many people prefer them. We would rather sell the thing nobody else explains than add a fourteenth guillotine to the internet.
