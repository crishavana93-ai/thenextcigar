Most travel cases make you pick a cut before you leave the house. This one does not: a flat cutter for parejos, a V for a deeper draw, and a punch for a torpedo, all in the same leather roll as four tubed cigars.

The tubes are the practical part. Four cigars in four sealed tubes travel better than four cigars sharing a pouch, and they survive a bag being sat on in a way a soft case does not.

## Who it is actually for

Not the person who owns one cutter and likes it. This is for the traveller who ends up smoking whatever the host offers, and would rather have the right tool for a figurado than saw at it with a guillotine.

It is also, plainly, the best-looking thing on our shelf when it is open on a table, which is not nothing when you are giving it to someone.
