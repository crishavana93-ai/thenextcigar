A jet flame is not a preference, it is physics. A soft flame drifts, and outdoors it drifts onto the wrapper. A jet holds its shape in wind, which is why every terrace smoker ends up owning one.

This one has two flame settings, which is the useful configuration: the wide setting for toasting a 54-ring foot evenly, the narrow one for touching up a burn that has gone lopsided without scorching the leaf around it.

## It arrives empty

Say it plainly, because it surprises people: **this lighter ships with no gas in it.** That is not a defect and it is not the seller cutting corners. A lighter with butane in it is UN 1057, dangerous goods class 2.1, and it cannot travel by ordinary air freight. Purged and empty, it is just a piece of metal, and that is how every lighter bought online crosses a border.

You fill it yourself with any standard butane refill — a can costs a few euros in any tobacconist and lasts months. Refined butane, sold for lighters, not camping gas.
