The case for a multitool is the travel case. A guillotine for parejos, a punch for figurados, and a V-cut for the people who like a V-cut is three objects, and a three-finger case has room for none of them. One body that does all three is the whole argument for this piece.

## What we know, and how we know it

The supplier's listing describes a V-cutter, a double-blade guillotine and a punch in one block, and it is the same listing that appears in our own cart today at 294.94 kr. That is the extent of what we can say about it from here. We have not held one. Our unit was ordered on 14 September, and when it arrives this page gets our photographs, the weight on our scale, and a plain verdict on the blades — including whether it goes back.

Until then we do not quote a ring-gauge limit, a steel grade, a pivot material or a comparison to a Xikar, because we would be copying a listing, and you can read a listing yourself.

## What it is not

It is not a branded cutter with a lifetime warranty. If that is what you want, the cutters guide names three and links to them. This is the unbranded version of the idea at a lower price, and the trade is that the warranty is our fourteen-day return window rather than a maker's promise.
