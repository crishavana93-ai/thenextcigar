The three-finger case is the one that gets used. Five-finger cases stay at home; the three-finger goes in the jacket, because three cigars is what a weekend actually needs.

The cedar lining is the part that matters. Cedar is what humidors are built from — it holds a little moisture, it keeps the smell of leather off the wrapper, and it stops three cigars pressed together from trading notes by the second day.
