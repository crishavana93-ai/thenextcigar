# Why this framing instead of "first 100"

The original draft capped OG at the first 100 signups. Two problems with that:

1. Member 100 and member 101 look identical from the outside — same profile depth, same engagement, same dollars spent — but one is lifetime-free and one isn't. That creates a fairness wound that surfaces every time someone replies to a launch email asking "wait, where did the door close?"
2. The "first 100" framing makes every future referral email do a small awkward dance: "you missed the cap, sorry, here's a consolation tier." That undercuts the welcome you want a new member to feel.

The new framing solves both:

- **Cutoff is a date, not a count.** Everyone who joined before 1 June 2026 is OG. No ambiguity, no off-by-one fairness wound.
- **Earned path stays open after launch.** A member who joins on day 500 and builds a real profile + brings two friends earns the same badge. The credential is binary; no numbers means no one feels smaller for arriving later.
- **No "you missed it" emails ever need to go out.** The earned path is the answer to that question.
