Cris here.

Two things to tell you, and they're connected.

1) The Finder is live.

The whole reason we built The Next Cigar was that there was no fair way to compare Cuban prices across European retailers. Today we ship the answer: 5 retailers across Germany and Switzerland, scraped every 6 hours, with email alerts the moment a price drops or a SKU comes back in stock. UK and the rest of Europe are next.

You'll find it inside the member app at https://thenextcigar.com/lounge/app/finder/ — your watchlist, your deal feed, and a new "Picked for your taste" row that uses the flavor profile you filled in during onboarding. If you skipped that step, you'll see Editor's Picks for now and a link to complete your taste profile in 30 seconds.

The public catalogue (with full text search, brand chips, country filter) lives at https://thenextcigar.com/finder/ — share it with the friends you've been telling about us.

2) You're a Founder Member. Lifetime access, free.

Here's the thing: you joined when The Next Cigar was a working draft. You filled in a profile when there were almost no other members to match with. You trusted us before the Finder existed. That deserves more than a thank-you.

The first 100 Lounge members are now Founder Members. That means:

- Every paid feature we ever ship is free for you. Forever.
- A Founder Member badge on your profile, visible to everyone in the directory.
- Direct line to me (cris@thenextcigar.com) for anything you want to see built.
- Your name in the credits when we publish the 2026 wrap.

You don't have to do anything to claim it. The badge auto-appears on your profile next time you log in. If somehow you're seeing this and you're number 101+, sorry — but referrals get added to a separate "Originals" tier that's also lifetime-free, so introduce the people you'd want sitting beside you.

That's it. Go play with the Finder. The Cohiba Robustos drop alert is the one I'm most curious about — it's currently OOS at most retailers; whichever shop restocks first triggers the alert, and we'll see how fast they sell through.

Thanks for being here early,
Cris
The Next Cigar
https://thenextcigar.com
