# The Lounge — Instagram Carousel from Real App Screenshots

**Format:** 1080 × 1350 (Instagram 4:5), 8 slides
**Build in:** Canva (same template as your UK Tobacco Ban post)
**Goal:** Show the actual app, not mockups. "This is real. This is what you get."

---

## Visual rules (apply to every slide)

- **Background:** Solid dark navy `#1A1D2E` (matches Lounge brand)
- **Phone frame:** Centered, ~480px wide, with thin gold (`#C9A961`) border to mimic an iPhone outline
- **Screenshot:** Fill the phone screen, ~440px wide, 4px corner radius
- **Top stamp:** Top-right corner — `THENEXTCIGAR.COM` and `LOUNGE.THENEXTCIGAR.COM` in tiny red caps (matches your UK ban post)
- **Headline:** White serif (Playfair Display or Cormorant), bold, ~64pt, top-left or below the phone
- **Subhead:** White Helvetica/Inter, ~20pt, two lines max
- **Red accent:** Use `#D9342B` on 1-2 keywords per headline (your existing accent)
- **Slide counter (optional):** Tiny "1/8", "2/8", etc. in bottom-right gold

## Canva build steps (5 min if you have the template)

1. Open your existing UK Tobacco Ban Canva design
2. Duplicate it 8 times (one per slide)
3. For slides 2-7: drop your matching screenshot into the phone-frame element. Keep the phone frame from the original UK design — just swap the inside content.
4. Update headline + subhead per the brief above
5. Apply the red accent to the highlighted word in each headline
6. Slide 1 + Slide 8 use no screenshot — pure typography on navy
7. Export all 8 as PNG, 1080×1350
8. Upload in order to a new IG post

---

## Posting strategy

- **Day:** Wednesday or Thursday, 7pm CET (best B2B/lifestyle engagement window)
- **Cover slide (Slide 1)** is your grid thumbnail — make it strong
- **Stories follow-up** same day at 9pm: share the post to Stories with a "Link" sticker
- **Pin the post** for 7 days so it stays at the top of your grid
- **Pinned comment** with the URL goes up within 5 minutes of posting (the algorithm rewards comment activity in the first 30 min)
