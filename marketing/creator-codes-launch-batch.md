## Step 3 — How to explain the dashboard if they ask

Most creators just want the link and trust the math. If they want a deeper explanation, here's the answer:

> The dashboard at `/lounge/app/partners/` shows three things in real time:
>
> 1. **Members referred** — total signups attributed to your code, ever
> 2. **Earnings YTD** — your share of all recurring + one-time revenue, this year
> 3. **Pending payout** — what's accrued since your last payout date
>
> Plus a feed of the most recent 20 conversion events (signup, membership upgrade, accessory order) so you can see exactly which activity is driving it.
>
> Payouts go out monthly on the 1st via your preferred method (bank transfer or PayPal). I'll email you a payout statement each cycle.

## What NOT to say (common mistakes to avoid)

- **Don't promise specific earnings numbers.** "You'll make $500/month" sets expectations you can't control. Stick to the percentages.
- **Don't tell them they're exclusive UK / EU / etc.** UKCE and UKTCE are both UK codes — if either creator finds out the other has the same region, they'll feel lied to. Be upfront if it comes up: "I'm building a creator team, not a single-creator deal."
- **Don't make tier promises you can't honor.** Creator tier (35% recurring) is the offer for all four right now. If one of them outperforms by 10x, you can promote them to Ambassador (45%) later — but don't promise it on day one.
